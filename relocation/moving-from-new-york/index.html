Relocation · New York to Tennessee

Moving from New York to Nashville

New York buyers moving to Nashville usually come for three things: taxes, space and pace. At $1.5 million of income, a New York State resident pays about $102,750 in state income tax; living in New York City adds about $58,100 more. Tennessee charges neither.

  • State income tax on $1.5M: about $102,750 in New York vs. $0 in Tennessee
  • Property tax on a $3M home: about $39,000 at New York's average vs. $11,175 in Brentwood
  • Estate tax: $7.35M exemption in New York vs. none in Tennessee
  • Illustrative only; confirm with your tax advisor

Before you move

What New York buyers should know

01

Statutory residency.

New York can tax you as a resident if you keep a home there and spend more than 183 days in the state. Count days carefully in your move year and keep records.

02

The estate-tax cliff.

New York's estate tax exemption disappears entirely for estates more than 5% above it. Tennessee has no estate or inheritance tax.

03

Commute and travel.

Nashville International (BNA) has nonstop flights to LaGuardia, JFK and Newark, so many buyers keep New York business ties.

04

What your money buys.

A budget that buys a classic six in Manhattan or a house in Westchester can buy an estate on acreage in Brentwood or Franklin, or a walkable home near Green Hills.

FAQ

Moving from New York: questions, answered

How much tax would I save moving from New York to Tennessee?
On an illustrative $1.5 million income and a $3 million home, state income tax plus property tax runs about $199,850 a year in New York (including New York City tax) versus about $11,175 in Brentwood, a difference of about $188,675. Your numbers depend on deductions, where you live and how your income is earned, so confirm with a tax professional.
Does Tennessee have a state income tax?
No. Tennessee has no tax on wages or salaries, and the Hall tax on interest and dividends was fully repealed in 2021.
Does Tennessee have an estate or inheritance tax?
No. Tennessee repealed its inheritance tax in 2016. New York's rule: $7.35M exemption, with a cliff: estates more than 5% over it lose the exemption entirely. Top rate 16%.
What are property taxes in Brentwood and Franklin?
Tennessee assesses homes at 25% of appraised value. On a $3 million home that is about $11,175 a year in Brentwood, about $12,150 in Franklin, about $9,750 in unincorporated Williamson County and about $21,105 in Nashville's Urban Services District, at 2026 rates.
Where do buyers from New York usually move in the Nashville area?
New Yorkers often look at Green Hills, Belle Meade and 12 South for walkability and restaurants, and at Brentwood and Franklin for schools and land.

Sources: New York income tax brackets, surtaxes and estate-tax rules from the state revenue department and Tax Foundation 2026 data; property-tax effective rates from the Tax Foundation's 2026 table (ACS 2024); Tennessee rates from the Tennessee Department of Revenue, Williamson County, the cities of Brentwood and Franklin, and Metro Nashville (2026).
