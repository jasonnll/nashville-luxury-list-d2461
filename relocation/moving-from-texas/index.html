Moving from Texas to Nashville

Texas and Tennessee both have no state income tax, but property taxes are very different. Texas leans on property tax: at its 1.40% effective average, a $3 million home costs about $42,000 a year. In Brentwood the same value costs about $11,175.

  • State income tax on $1.5M: about $0 in Texas vs. $0 in Tennessee
  • Property tax on a $3M home: about $42,000 at Texas's average vs. $11,175 in Brentwood
  • Estate tax: None in Texas vs. none in Tennessee
  • Illustrative only; confirm with your tax advisor

Before you move

What Texas buyers should know

01

Property tax.

The biggest yearly difference between the two states. Tennessee's 25% assessment ratio keeps bills low.

02

Sales tax.

Both states lean on sales tax. Tennessee's combined rate is 9.25% in Davidson County and 9.75% in Williamson.

03

Weather.

Milder summers and more rain and green; winters are short.

04

What you give up.

Nothing on income tax, and you keep the no-estate-tax status.

FAQ

Moving from Texas: questions, answered

How much tax would I save moving from Texas to Tennessee?
On an illustrative $1.5 million income and a $3 million home, state income tax plus property tax runs about $42,000 a year in Texas versus about $11,175 in Brentwood, a difference of about $30,825. Your numbers depend on deductions, where you live and how your income is earned, so confirm with a tax professional.
Does Tennessee have a state income tax?
No. Tennessee has no tax on wages or salaries, and the Hall tax on interest and dividends was fully repealed in 2021.
Does Tennessee have an estate or inheritance tax?
No. Tennessee repealed its inheritance tax in 2016. Texas's rule: None.
What are property taxes in Brentwood and Franklin?
Tennessee assesses homes at 25% of appraised value. On a $3 million home that is about $11,175 a year in Brentwood, about $12,150 in Franklin, about $9,750 in unincorporated Williamson County and about $21,105 in Nashville's Urban Services District, at 2026 rates.
Where do buyers from Texas usually move in the Nashville area?
Texas buyers often land in Brentwood and Franklin for schools and space, and in Green Hills, 12 South and East Nashville for walkable living.
