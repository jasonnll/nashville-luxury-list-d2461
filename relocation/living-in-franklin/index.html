Living in Franklin: a local's guide

Franklin pairs a nationally recognized historic Main Street with rolling farmland. Locals shop the Saturday farmers market at The Factory, walk Harlinsdale Farm and Pinkerton Park, eat at GRAYS on Main and Puckett's, and drive out to Leiper's Fork on weekends, with homes ranging from downtown Victorians to Westhaven and the golf communities of College Grove.

  • A Great American Main Street, honored by the National Trust
  • Williamson County Schools and Franklin Special School District
  • Cool Springs offices and shopping on the east side
  • About 30 minutes to downtown Nashville

Welcome to Franklin

A note before you arrive

Franklin has the kind of downtown people build movie sets to imitate: Victorian storefronts, the restored 1937 Franklin Theatre and a town square where neighbors still run into each other.

Step outside the historic core and you'll find walkable new towns like Westhaven, horse country in Leiper's Fork and golf communities in College Grove. For taxes, schools and market data, see the Nashville Relocation Playbook and the Franklin neighborhood guide.

FAQ

Living in Franklin: questions, answered

Is Franklin, TN a good place to live?
Franklin is one of Middle Tennessee's most popular places to live for its historic Main Street, community events, Williamson County schools and mix of homes, from downtown Victorians to Westhaven and the estates of Leiper's Fork and College Grove.
What is there to do in Franklin?
Locals shop and dine on historic Main Street, catch shows at the Franklin Theatre, visit Carnton and the Carter House, walk Harlinsdale Farm and Pinkerton Park, shop the Saturday farmers market at The Factory and spend weekend afternoons in Leiper's Fork.
How far is Franklin from Nashville?
Downtown Franklin is about 30 minutes from downtown Nashville by car via I-65 outside rush hour. Cool Springs, on Franklin's east side, is closer.
Which school district serves Franklin?
Most of Franklin is served by Williamson County Schools. Inside its boundaries, the Franklin Special School District serves grades K-8, with high school through Williamson County Schools. Always confirm zoning for a specific address.
What are the best restaurants in downtown Franklin?
Local favorites include GRAYS on Main, Culaccino, Saint Goose and Red Pony for dinner, Puckett's and Merridee's Breadbasket for casual meals, and Honest Coffee Roasters, Hank's Coffee Station and Stable Reserve for coffee.
