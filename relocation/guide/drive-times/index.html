Quick answers

How long is the drive from Brentwood to downtown Nashville?

About 20 minutes off-peak and roughly 32 minutes at rush hour, mostly on I-65.

How long is the drive from Franklin to downtown Nashville?

About 30 minutes off-peak from downtown Franklin and roughly 48 minutes at rush hour.

How far is Green Hills from the airport?

About 20 minutes off-peak to BNA.
