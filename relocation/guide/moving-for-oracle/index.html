Oracle in Nashville

Oracle held its groundbreaking for a $4.5 billion global headquarters on the East Bank in November 2026, with a campus that also plans an amphitheater, a hotel and a pedestrian bridge. Until it opens, many teams work from Oracle’s expanded offices in the Neuhoff District, which overlook the construction, and the company has also had employees in Capitol View.

Oracle has said it aims to have about 8,500 employees in Nashville. Reporting this year noted it has offered relocation incentives to some existing employees, so ask HR exactly what your package covers. See Your Relocation Package, Decoded.

Want Williamson County schools?

The closest Williamson options to this office: Brentwood (22 min off-peak, 35 at rush), Governors Club (27 min off-peak, 43 at rush), Nolensville (28 min off-peak, 45 at rush). See Brentwood and Franklin.

Quick answers

What is the best neighborhood for Oracle employees?

For the shortest commute to East Bank campus (under construction): East Nashville, Germantown, 12 South. Families wanting Williamson County public schools often choose Brentwood or Franklin and accept a longer drive.
