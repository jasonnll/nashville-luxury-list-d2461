Three ways to get a new home

Fastest

Buy a finished or nearly finished spec home

A builder’s home on an infill lot in Green Hills, Belle Meade, Forest Hills or Brentwood, or in a new community. You can often still pick finishes if you buy early.

Most control

Custom build on your own lot

Buy the land (or a teardown), hire an architect and a builder, and design every detail. Expect 12–24 months and a contingency for changes.

Amenities

Build in a community

Choose a lot and a plan in a golf or amenity community like The Grove, Troubadour or Westhaven, often with an approved-builder list.

Where new construction is happening

  • In-town infill: Green Hills, Belle Meade, Forest Hills, Sylvan Park and 12 South, where older homes are replaced.
  • Brentwood: rebuilds on one-acre lots and newer estate neighborhoods toward the south and east.
  • Franklin and College Grove: new phases in amenity communities, plus custom farms around Leiper’s Fork and Arrington.

What to watch for

  • The builder’s contract is written for the builder. Have your own agent, and an attorney for custom builds, review deposits, allowances, change-order pricing and delay terms.
  • Inspect anyway. Independent inspections at foundation, pre-drywall and final catch problems while they are still cheap to fix.
  • Allowances and change orders are where budgets slip. Price your finish selections before you sign.
  • Appraisals on custom homes can lag the cost; plan for a gap.
  • Warranty: know what is covered and schedule an 11-month walk-through.
  • Lot due diligence: topography, drainage, septic or sewer, tree ordinances and HOA design review.

Quick answers

How long does it take to build a custom luxury home in Nashville?

Plan on roughly 12 to 24 months from design to move-in, depending on size, permitting, lot work and finish selections.

Do I need my own agent to buy new construction?

It is wise. The builder’s sales team represents the builder; your own agent negotiates price, upgrades and terms for you, and is usually paid by the builder.
