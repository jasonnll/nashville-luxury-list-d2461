Franklin neighborhoods

Where to look in Franklin

$1.5M–$4M

Historic Downtown Franklin

Restored Victorians and new homes a few blocks from Main Street’s shops, restaurants and the Franklin Theatre. Franklin Special School District (PK–8) in much of the area, then Franklin High.

$1.3M–$3M

Westhaven

A walkable town-within-a-town with a golf club, pools, a town center and front porches on every block. Williamson County Schools, including a school inside the neighborhood (verify zoning).

$2M–$15M+

Leiper’s Fork & West Franklin

Rolling farmland, horse properties and a tiny village of galleries and music: the land-and-privacy answer. Williamson County Schools; many families also commute to BGA or Brentwood Academy.

$1.2M–$3M

Thompson’s Station

Small-town charm south of Franklin with newer communities, farm parcels and more home for the money. Williamson County Schools, often Independence High (verify by address).

$2M–$10M+

College Grove

Farms, wineries and private golf communities like The Grove and Troubadour in southern Williamson County. Williamson County Schools (often Page High) or private schools in Franklin and Brentwood.
