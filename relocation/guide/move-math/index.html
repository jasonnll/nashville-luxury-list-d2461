Quick answers

Does Tennessee have a state income tax?

Tennessee does not tax wages or salaries, and the Hall tax on investment income was fully repealed in 2021.

What is the property tax on a $3 million home in Brentwood?

At the 2026 combined rate of $1.49 per $100 of assessed value (homes are assessed at 25%), about $11,175 a year. In Nashville’s Urban Services District ($2.814) it is about $21,105.
