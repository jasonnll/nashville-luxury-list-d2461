For decades the land across the Cumberland from downtown was parking lots and industry. Now it is the biggest redevelopment in Nashville’s history.

What’s happening

  • The new Nissan Stadium remains on track for a February 2027 completion; the current stadium is to be phased out after the 2026 season.
  • Oracle’s campus is under construction.
  • A 600-room hotel beside the stadium is now expected to start construction in Q3 2027 and open in Q3 2030, after the Super Bowl.
  • New streets, housing, parks and a pedestrian boulevard are being phased in by the East Bank Development Authority.

What it means if you are buying

East Nashville, Germantown and the urban-core condo market sit closest to the action. Expect more restaurants, more jobs and, on event days, more traffic. Buyers who want quiet look a little farther out; buyers who want energy and long-term upside look closer in.
