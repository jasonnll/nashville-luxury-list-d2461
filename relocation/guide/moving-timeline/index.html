Ask Jason a question
90 days before

Plan the search

Talk through neighborhoods and schools, get jumbo pre-approval and start private school applications if needed.

75 days before

Scouting trip

Spend two or three days touring areas and homes. Use Your Perfect Day to plan it.

60 days before

Go under contract

Write the offer, schedule inspections and lock your rate. List your current home if selling.

45 days before

Book movers

Get three quotes; long-distance movers book up, especially in summer.

30 days before

Schools and records

Enroll by address and request transcripts and immunization records.

14 days before

Utilities and internet

Schedule start dates for closing day and book internet installation.

7 days before

Final details

Confirm closing time, verify wire instructions by phone (wire fraud is real) and schedule the final walkthrough.

Move day

Closing and move-in

Sign, get your keys and rekey the locks. Welcome home.

7 days after

First week

Work through The First 90 Days checklist.

30 days after

Make it official

Driver’s license, vehicle registration and voter registration.
