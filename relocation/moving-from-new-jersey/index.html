Moving from New Jersey to Nashville

New Jersey combines a 10.75% top income tax rate with some of the highest property taxes in the country. For a household earning $1.5 million in a $3 million home, the move to Brentwood cuts the combined bill by roughly $170,000 a year.

  • State income tax on $1.5M: about $126,000 in New Jersey vs. $0 in Tennessee
  • Property tax on a $3M home: about $56,400 at New Jersey's average vs. $11,175 in Brentwood
  • Estate tax: No estate tax in New Jersey vs. none in Tennessee
  • Illustrative only; confirm with your tax advisor

Before you move

What New Jersey buyers should know

01

Property tax is the headline.

At New Jersey's 1.88% effective average, a $3 million home costs about $56,400 a year in property tax. In Brentwood the same value costs about $11,175.

02

Leaving New Jersey.

New Jersey's exit ("bulk sale") rules require a filing and an estimated-tax payment when nonresident sellers sell real property. Plan for it at closing.

03

Commute and travel.

BNA has nonstop flights to Newark and the New York airports.

04

Schools.

Williamson County Schools are a major draw, and Nashville has a deep bench of private schools.

FAQ

Moving from New Jersey: questions, answered

How much tax would I save moving from New Jersey to Tennessee?
On an illustrative $1.5 million income and a $3 million home, state income tax plus property tax runs about $182,400 a year in New Jersey versus about $11,175 in Brentwood, a difference of about $171,225. Your numbers depend on deductions, where you live and how your income is earned, so confirm with a tax professional.
Does Tennessee have a state income tax?
No. Tennessee has no tax on wages or salaries, and the Hall tax on interest and dividends was fully repealed in 2021.
Does Tennessee have an estate or inheritance tax?
No. Tennessee repealed its inheritance tax in 2016. New Jersey's rule: No estate tax, but an inheritance tax of up to 16% on transfers to some heirs (spouses, children and grandchildren are exempt).
What are property taxes in Brentwood and Franklin?
Tennessee assesses homes at 25% of appraised value. On a $3 million home that is about $11,175 a year in Brentwood, about $12,150 in Franklin, about $9,750 in unincorporated Williamson County and about $21,105 in Nashville's Urban Services District, at 2026 rates.
Where do buyers from New Jersey usually move in the Nashville area?
New Jersey buyers often focus on Brentwood and Franklin for schools and land, and on Green Hills for a shorter drive to the city.
