Relocating to Nashville? The playbook for luxury buyers moving to Middle Tennessee

Middle Tennessee is not one luxury market. It is a dozen small ones split across two counties with different tax rates and school systems. At $1.5M and above, most relocating buyers choose between Davidson County's estate areas (Belle Meade, Forest Hills, Oak Hill, Green Hills) and Williamson County: Brentwood, Franklin and College Grove, where property tax runs about half. Jason Kloess of Compass helps you pick the right one and buy it with a single, well-planned trip.

Chapters 02 & 03 · Taxes

How much will you save on taxes by moving to Tennessee?

For a high-earning household, state income tax is often the largest single line that changes with a move. Tennessee does not tax earned or investment income. Property tax is where the county line matters: Tennessee assesses homes at 25% of appraised value, then applies the local rate per $100.

Relocation FAQ

Questions buyers ask before moving to Nashville

What are the best areas near Nashville for luxury buyers who are relocating?

At $1.5M and above, most relocating buyers choose between Davidson County estate areas (Belle Meade, Forest Hills, Oak Hill and Green Hills, about 12 to 15 minutes from downtown) and Williamson County: Brentwood for one-acre lots about 20 to 25 minutes south, Franklin and Leiper's Fork for historic downtown living and estate farms about 30 to 35 minutes south, and College Grove for gated golf and acreage about 40 to 45 minutes out. Williamson County carries roughly half the property tax rate of Davidson County.

Does Tennessee have a state income tax?

No. Tennessee does not tax wages, salary, bonuses, interest, dividends or capital gains for individuals. The Hall income tax on investment income was fully repealed as of 2021, and Tennessee repealed its inheritance tax in 2016. The state relies on sales tax instead: 9.25% combined in Davidson County and 9.75% in Williamson County. Confirm your situation with a CPA.

How much is property tax on a $3 million home in Nashville, Brentwood or Franklin?

Tennessee assesses residential property at 25% of appraised value and applies the local rate per $100 of assessed value. On a $3M home that is about $21,105 a year in Davidson County's Urban Services District, about $20,865 in the General Services District (Belle Meade, Forest Hills, Oak Hill, plus any city levy), about $11,175 in Brentwood, about $12,150 in Franklin and about $9,750 in unincorporated Williamson County such as College Grove, based on 2026 rates.

What do luxury homes cost in Nashville, Brentwood and Franklin in 2026?

Recent Redfin medians across all home types: Belle Meade about $4.30M (three months to August 2026), Forest Hills about $2.30M, Brentwood about $1.42M, Green Hills about $1.25M and Franklin about $863K citywide. Typical luxury ranges run $1.5M to $8M+ in Belle Meade, $1.2M to $6M+ in Brentwood, $1.5M to $7M+ in Franklin and $1.5M to $10M+ in College Grove and Leiper's Fork.

Is it a buyer's or seller's market for Nashville luxury homes right now?

As of fall 2026 the upper end of Middle Tennessee is more balanced than in 2021 and 2022. There were about 2,289 active listings at $1M+ against roughly 460 closings a month, about five months of supply, and 43% of active regional listings were priced below their original ask. The best-located and best-finished homes still sell quickly, often as Compass Private Exclusives or quietly between agents.

Can I buy a home in Nashville without visiting first?

Most of the work can happen remotely. Jason Kloess starts with a 30 to 45 minute Virtual Relocation Consult, then narrows homes with live video tours, and most buyers decide after one two- to three-day scouting trip. Tennessee closings are handled by a title company or closing attorney, and with lender approval you can sign by mail or electronically.

How long does a luxury relocation to Nashville take?

Most luxury relocations take six to twelve months from the first conversation to move-in. Independent school application deadlines usually fall in winter, so families often work backward from a school start or job date. New residents must get a Tennessee driver license within 30 days.

Who pays the buyer's agent when relocating to Nashville?

Since the 2024 NAR settlement, buyers sign a written agreement with their agent before touring that states the agent's compensation. That compensation is negotiable and is often paid by the seller as a negotiated term, but it is not guaranteed. Jason explains the options on your first call.
