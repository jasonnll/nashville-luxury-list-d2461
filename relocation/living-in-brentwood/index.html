Living in Brentwood: a local's guide

Brentwood is where Nashville goes to exhale. Just over the Davidson County line in Williamson County, it trades city bustle for big lots, a connected greenway system and well-regarded public schools. Life centers on Crockett Park, Smith Park and Ravenswood Mansion, the club and neighborhood favorites, with downtown Nashville about 20 minutes up I-65.

  • Williamson County Schools, plus Brentwood Academy and Currey Ingram
  • Acre-plus lots, gated enclaves and estate streets
  • Paved greenways linking the city's parks
  • About 20 minutes to downtown Nashville

Welcome to Brentwood

A note before you arrive

Life in Brentwood centers on the parks, the club and the Friday-night game. Summer evenings mean lawn chairs at Crockett Park, and weekends start on the greenway.

This guide covers the places locals actually go. For property taxes, schools and the market, see the Nashville Relocation Playbook and the Brentwood neighborhood guide.

FAQ

Living in Brentwood: questions, answered

Is Brentwood, TN a good place to live?
Brentwood is one of Middle Tennessee's most sought-after suburbs for its large lots, connected parks and greenways, Williamson County Schools and easy access to Nashville. It is quieter and more residential than Nashville or downtown Franklin.
What is there to do in Brentwood?
Locals spend time at Crockett Park, which hosts summer concerts and the July 4th fireworks, Marcella Vivrette Smith Park and Ravenswood Mansion, Tower Park and the Deerwood Arboretum, plus shopping at Hill Center Brentwood and Maryland Farms.
How far is Brentwood from downtown Nashville?
About 20 minutes by car via I-65 outside rush hour. Green Hills and the Cool Springs area of Franklin are each roughly 10 to 15 minutes away.
What schools serve Brentwood?
Public schools are part of Williamson County Schools, including Brentwood High and Ravenwood High. Private options include Brentwood Academy and Currey Ingram Academy, with Franklin Road Academy just north. Always confirm school zoning for a specific address.
Where do people eat in Brentwood?
Local favorites include Mere Bulles, HoneyFire BBQ and Judge Bean's BBQ, The Puffy Muffin for breakfast, and Crema, The Well Coffeehouse and The Perch for coffee.
