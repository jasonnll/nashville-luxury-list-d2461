Living in Nashville: a local's guide

Nashville is a big-hearted city that still feels like a collection of small towns. Locals spend weekends at Radnor Lake, the Warner Parks and Cheekwood, shop the Richland Park and East Nashville farmers markets, and eat at chef-driven spots like Audrey, Bastion and Rolf and Daughters, with neighborhoods ranging from Belle Meade estates to walkable 12 South.

  • No state income tax on wages in Tennessee
  • BNA airport about 15 minutes from downtown
  • Neighborhoods from estate streets to walkable cafe districts
  • Live music every night, from the Ryman to neighborhood listening rooms

Welcome to Nashville

A note before you arrive

Ten minutes in any direction gets you something different: tree-canopied estate streets in Belle Meade, sidewalk cafes in 12 South, old brick and new kitchens in Germantown, porch culture in East Nashville.

This guide skips the spreadsheets. It's the short list Jason gives friends who are moving here: the places locals actually go, the parks people spend Saturdays in, and the neighborhoods worth a slow drive. For taxes, schools and market data, see the Nashville Relocation Playbook.

FAQ

Living in Nashville: questions, answered

What is it like to live in Nashville?
Nashville feels like a collection of small towns inside a fast-growing city. Locals split their time between parks like Radnor Lake and the Warner Parks, neighborhood restaurants and coffee shops, and live music most nights. Tennessee has no state income tax on wages.
What are the best neighborhoods in Nashville for luxury buyers?
Belle Meade, Green Hills and Forest Hills/Oak Hill are the traditional estate areas, while 12 South, Sylvan Park and East Nashville offer walkable living. Brentwood and Franklin, just south in Williamson County, are the other common choices.
Where are the farmers markets in Nashville?
The Nashville Farmers' Market in Germantown is open daily. Richland Park in Sylvan Park runs Saturdays year-round, East Nashville runs Tuesdays from spring through December, and 12 South runs Tuesday evenings in season at Sevier Park. Check each market's site for current hours.
What are the best restaurants in Nashville?
Locals' short list includes Audrey, Bastion, Rolf and Daughters, Bourbon Steak and Momotaro for special dinners, and Arnold's Country Kitchen, Loveless Cafe and Prince's Hot Chicken for Nashville classics.
Does Tennessee have a state income tax?
No. Tennessee does not tax wages or salaries, and the Hall tax on interest and dividends was repealed in 2021. See the Relocation Playbook for property tax by county.
