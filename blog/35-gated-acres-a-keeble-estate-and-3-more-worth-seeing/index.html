35 Gated Acres, a Keeble Estate, and 3 More Worth Seeing

By Jason Kloess · October 23, 2026

Top 5 Homes This Week + Off Markets

This week brought some of the most interesting listings we’ve seen all fall. There’s a 1940 Edwin Keeble estate on five and a half acres in Forest Hills, a 35 acre gated property in Thompson’s Station with room for horses and a car collection, and a fully renovated home in Horseshoe Bend with Warner Parks as its backyard. If you’ve been waiting for something with real land, real character, or both, this is the week to pay attention.

With more than a third of Williamson County's active listings carrying at least one price reduction — averaging nearly $139,000 off the original ask — sellers are adjusting to a market that no longer forgives aggressive pricing.

For buyers, the calculus is clear: inventory is there, sellers are motivated, and the Compass Coming Soon pipeline this week shows multiple estate-tier listings preparing to hit — which means the most compelling opportunities will close before public launch. The 37205 corridor in Nashville continues to hold values well, and Franklin's sub-$2M segment remains tightly sought.

— Jason Kloess | Nashville Luxury List | Compass Nashville
c: 615-457-0768

4810 SCENIC OAKS LN, THOMPSON STATION, TN 37179

5 BEDS / 6 FULL 1 HALF BATHS, 10,996 SQFT 35 ACRES

Thirty five gated acres of rolling Tennessee countryside, a picturesque pond, and a Georgian estate wrapped in a 72 foot porch. Inside are five fireplaces, a main level primary suite, a whiskey room, a media room, and two climate controlled sleeping porches upstairs, with an elevator connecting every level. The gym, pool, 12 person spa, and lighted pickleball court make this a place you rarely need to leave. Collectors will appreciate five garage spaces at the house plus a car barn for nine more. Three additional recorded build sites open the door to a family compound, a working farm, or a true legacy property. A 20 acre option is also available at $9,995,000. - $11,995,000

7035 HYDE PARK TRAIL, COLLEGE GROVE, TN 37046

6 BEDS / 6 FULL 1 HALF BATHS, 6,000 SQFT

If you’ve been searching for the right home and keep coming up short, this is your chance to build it from the ground up. Hyde Park Estates is a gated community in College Grove offering homesites of five acres or more, each paired with a fully custom home by Partners in Building. You choose the layout, the outdoor living, and the entertaining spaces, all designed around the way you actually live. You get the privacy of real acreage while staying minutes from Franklin, golf, dining, and top rated Williamson County schools. Homesites of this size and quality are getting harder to find every year. - $3,900,000

VIEW LISTING

110 POPLAR ST, FRANKLIN, TN 37064

5 BEDS / 5 FULL 1 HALF BATHS, 7,337 SQFT

Over an acre with no HOA, directly across from Berry Farms, is a rare find in Franklin. This extensively renovated home offers multiple bonus spaces, a private guest suite, and a kitchen with quartz counters, a gas range, and double ovens. A detached 1,227 square foot studio with a gym gives you room to work, train, or expand. The circular drive fits 10 or more vehicles, with space for boats and RVs. Out back, a brick privacy fence surrounds a heated saltwater pool you can use well beyond summer. Downtown Franklin, Cool Springs, and I 65 are all close by. - $2,489,000

VIEW LISTING

2966 POLO CLUB RD, NASHVILLE, TN 37221

5 BEDS / 3 FULL 1 HALF BATHS, 4,151 SQFT

Horseshoe Bend sits between the Harpeth and Little Harpeth Rivers, with the 3,100 acre Warner Parks as your back fence. You get a Nashville address with Williamson County schools, and you’re minutes from CPA and Ensworth and twenty from downtown. The home has been completely renovated with a new kitchen, new baths, new HVAC, new maple hardwoods, and fully redone landscaping. The main level primary suite has its own private entrance, opens to the back deck, and features a soaking tub set in a sunlit bay window. A four car garage leaves room for a home gym or cold plunge. Neighborhood trails connect directly to more than fifty miles of park hiking. - $1,672,853

VIEW LISTING

6129 HILLSBORO PIKE, NASHVILLE, TN 37215

6 BEDS / 4 FULL 1 HALF BATHS, 6,347 SQFT ON 5.56 ACRES

Cedarwood Estate sits on five and a half private, level acres in the heart of Forest Hills and was designed by renowned Nashville architect Edwin Keeble. You arrive through a gated entrance framed by a dry stacked fieldstone wall, limestone pillars, and custom Herndon and Merry ironwork, then follow a circular drive redesigned by Gavin Duke. Inside, preserved architectural character meets thoughtful updates, including a reconfigured kitchen with TRUE column refrigeration and a custom Hector Finch chandelier. The grounds include a barn, a private walking trail, and Forest Hills approved plans for a pool and pool house. The estate takes its name from historic cedars in the front yard that are roughly 250 years old. Acreage with this kind of provenance this close to Green Hills almost never comes to market. - $6,965,000

5715 GREENBRIAR RD, FRANKLIN, TN 37064

4 BEDS / 4 FULL 1 HALF BATHS, 120 ACRES

Welcome to Cortina Farms, an exquisite 120-acre estate nestled near Leipers Fork in the picturesque town of Franklin, TN. This unparalleled property effortlessly transports you to the heart of Italy w/its masterful stonework & enchanting lavender-lined entryway. The estate features a charming main house set back to capture breathtaking views, accompanied by guest houses that provide ample space for visitors. The property boasts a myriad of luxurious amenities, including a wellness center, a party barn w/commercial catering kitchen & 2 pristine lakes stocked with bass & catfish, perfect for leisurely afternoons of fishing. Equestrian enthusiasts will be delighted by the state-of-the-art 10-stall equestrian barn, ensuring your horses enjoy the same luxury as you. - $30,000,000

412 WILD ELM ST, FRANKLIN, TN 37064

6 BEDS / 6 FULL 1 HALF BATHS, 7,169 SQFT

Set on one of the most coveted streets in prestigious Westhaven, 412 Wild Elm is a showpiece of design and craftsmanship. This fully renovated 7,169-square-foot residence has been reimagined from top to bottom, with every square inch thoughtfully touched to create a home that feels straight from the pages of a luxury design magazine. Two expansive patio spaces provide the perfect settings for outdoor dining, lounging, and gathering with friends, while the yard offers ample space for a future pool. A 3-car garage adds convenience and storage. - $3,799,000

VIEW LISTING

9616 DEER TRACK CT, BRENTWOOD, TN 37027

5 BEDS / 5 FULL 1 HALF BATHS, 5,938 SQFT

A rare combination in Brentwood: nearly 6,000 SF with the flexibility for multi-generational living, a 5-car side-entry garage (room for up to 7), a framed elevator shaft ready for installation, and a level cul-de-sac lot with room for a pool. The big-ticket items are done: all HVAC units and a tankless water heater were installed in 2025. Collectors and hobbyists will appreciate the side-entry garage, with brand-new epoxy floors, space for up to seven vehicles, and room for a workshop. - $2,100,000
