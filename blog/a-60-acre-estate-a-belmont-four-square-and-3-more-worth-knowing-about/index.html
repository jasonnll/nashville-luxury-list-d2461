A 60 Acre Estate, a Belmont Four Square, and 3 More Worth Knowing About

By Jason Kloess · October 9, 2026

Top 5 Homes This Week + Off Markets

Hi everyone, what a wild week in Nashville real estate! Even with rates holding firm, inventory is moving and luxury homes are selling. Above $2M, demand is unmistakably strengthening. Closings hit a 4-year weekly high of 108 transactions, and contracts rose 8% year-over-year, upper-bracket buyers are active, decisive, and absorbing inventory.

Brentwood stands apart from the broader metro trend: 3.8 months of supply and a 98.3% list-to-sale ratio keep sellers firmly in command. By contrast, Franklin's $3M+ tier is moving at 81 days to contract with a 34.7% attrition rate, pricing precision and patient strategy are the difference between a deal and a delisting. Interestingly, despite elevated supply, the price-reduction rate in the $1M–$2M band hit a 4-year low at 31.2%,  sellers are holding firm, but buyers have time and selection on their side.

This week brought a few homes that don’t come along often. There’s a 60 acre hilltop estate in Williamson County, two new builds you can see on Compass before they reach other sites, and a rooftop in East Nashville with one of the best downtown views around. Several are still under construction, so there’s time to make finish choices your own. Take a look below, and reply here if one catches your eye.

c: 615-457-0768

1770 MOLLY HOLLOW RD, NOLENSVILLE, TN 37135

5 BEDS / 5 FULL 4 HALF BATHS, 12,414 SQFT 61 ACRES

Sixty acres at the end of a private dead end road, minutes from Cool Springs and Brentwood. The main residence, designed by MK Studio and still to be built, covers more than 12,000 square feet and is set to look over a private pond and long views of the rolling hills. The land has walking and ATV trails, open pasture, woods, and a herd of albino deer the property is known for. A ridge top fire pit catches the sunsets, and the plan allows for a five bedroom guest house and a barn. Another roughly 105 adjoining acres may also be available, which could make this a 165 acre family estate. - $15,125,000

900 ALDER DR, NASHVILLE, TN 37220

6 BEDS / 7 FULL 2 HALF BATHS, 9,826 SQFT

Maker Construction and Allard Ward Architects teamed up on this new home, wrapped in hand laid stone on a full acre in Oak Hill. The great room has a vaulted white oak ceiling, a stone fireplace from floor to ceiling, and a wall of French doors. The kitchen has two islands, a 60 inch range, a scullery, a bar, and wine storage. The walkout lower level has a gym, a sauna, a cold plunge, and a massage room. Out back are a pool, a hot tub, and a pool house with a full bath, plus an elevator inside. - $7,500,000

1105 GLENVIEW DR, NASHVILLE, TN 37206

4 BEDS / 3 FULL 1 HALF BATHS, 4,126 SQFT

A California style contemporary five minutes from Broadway, with more than $275,000 in upgrades. It opens with a mahogany pivot door, then floor to ceiling pocket doors, white oak floors, and handmade Zellige tile. There are two primary suites, and the whole house runs on Control4 with Bang and Olufsen audio and a home theater. Outside you’ll find a gated entry, a motorized pergola, a spa, and a rooftop deck facing the downtown skyline. Shelby Park and the new East Bank are right next door. Open house this Sunday from 2 to 4 PM. - $1,795,000

1804 CEDAR LN, NASHVILLE, TN 37212

7 BEDS / 7 FULL 1 HALF BATHS, 6,145 SQFT

New construction in walkable Belmont is hard to find, and this one has the look of a classic Four Square. Vernich Interiors designed the inside, which includes a formal dining room, two fireplaces, a separate primary wing, and a large upstairs rec room with a wet bar. A one bedroom apartment over the three car garage works as a guest suite, office, or pool house. Out back is a gunite pool with a hot tub and tanning ledge, plus a covered porch with a fireplace. Delivery is expected in spring 2027. - $4,500,000

5111 CORNWALL DR, BRENTWOOD, TN 37027

6 BEDS / 7 FULL 2 HALF BATHS, 10,736 SQFT

Kaeser Building is building this custom home on more than an acre in Meadow Lake, zoned for Brentwood’s top rated schools. MK Studio designed the floor plan with the primary and guest suites both on the main level. The kitchen has two islands, a scullery, and an oversized pantry. A two story great room and a dining room with wood beams open to a covered porch over a pool with a sun shelf and spa. There’s also a wellness center, a pool lounge with a wet bar, a bonus room with a bunk room, a porte cochere, and a four car garage. It’s still under construction, so the next owner can make it their own. - $8,995,000

5 BEDS / 5 FULL 2 HALF BATHS, 7,712 SQFT

Stunning custom luxury residence in West Meade, ideally positioned on coveted Bresslyn Road in one of Nashville’s most desirable settings. Set on nearly one acre, this exceptional new construction home offers more than 7,700 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space with 5 bedrooms, 5.5 baths, a 4-car garage, custom pool by Oasis Pools, and expansive second-floor flex space. Refined interiors pair timeless architecture with elevated finishes, including sand-and-finish white oak hardwood floors, quartzite and marble surfaces, designer lighting, floating stairs, and distinctive interior stonework throughout the entry and fireplace. - $6,000,000
