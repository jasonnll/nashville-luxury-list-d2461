Two homes you can see before anyone else

By Jason Kloess · October 16, 2026

Top 5 Homes This Week + Off Markets

The two-speed luxury market in Middle Tennessee is getting sharper. Nashville's $2M+ inventory sits above six months of supply — a meaningful buyer's market where 68% of sellers are taking discounts, with a median haircut of 5.3% off original list. Buyers with pre-approval and flexibility have real pricing power in Davidson County this fall, particularly in the $2M–$4M range where the discount rate peaks.

Williamson County tells the opposite story. Brentwood is holding at 3.8 months of supply with a 98.3% list-to-sale ratio — sellers here are not discounting, and new construction (which made up 45% of $2M+ closings over the past year) is continuing to set price ceilings as builders price aggressively into the $3M–$9M range. Franklin's luxury pipeline is active this week, with multiple Coming Soon listings appearing on Compass ahead of MLS exposure.

The off-market advantage is real: Compass pre-market data shows that properties launched through Coming Soon and Private Exclusive programs sell faster and closer to ask than MLS-first launches. If one catches your eye, reply to this email and we’ll get you in for a private showing before the weekend crowd.

720 ADKISSON LN, NASHVILLE, TN 37205

5 BEDS / 5 FULL 2 HALF BATHS, 6,911 SQFT

Nearly an acre in West Meade, minutes from Hillwood Country Club and an easy drive downtown. This brand new contemporary by TimoSIX has solid white oak floors, custom Amish built cabinetry, leathered quartzite and oversized windows that keep the modern design feeling warm. The kitchen is set up for serious hosting, with panel ready Thermador appliances, a wine cellar, a wet bar and a walk in pantry. Outside is where it really stands apart: more than 3,500 square feet of balconies and patios, a heated pool, and a pool bar with its own grill. Land, space and proximity to town rarely come together like this, and it returns to the market priced well below its spring debut. - $3,275,000

This new Patterson plan in June Lake has soaring ceilings, a curved staircase and a vaulted primary bedroom and bath. Two bedrooms on the main level make hosting family and guests easy, and a wet bar helps with entertaining. A porte cochere with a three car garage gives the home a grand arrival. June Lake is built around walking to dinner, with 1.2 million square feet of shops and restaurants planned within the community, and the home is zoned for Summit High. - $1,420,510

4807 DAKOTA AVE, NASHVILLE, TN 37209

6 BEDS / 4 FULL 1 HALF BATHS, 4,143 SQFT

Built in 2022 and designed with real restraint: white oak, plaster, limewash and soft linen, with nothing shouting for attention. The primary suite is on the main level, with a renovated spa bath, a freestanding tub and a closet that has its own washer and dryer. The kitchen is made for cooking, with Fisher & Paykel appliances, cabinetry to the ceiling and an island that seats four. Above the detached garage is a vaulted studio with exposed beams, a kitchenette and a full bath, a natural fit for an office, guests or both. It sits on a quiet street zoned for Sylvan Park Paideia, a short walk to McCabe Park and the greenway, and the price was just adjusted. - $2,150,000

5 BEDS / 6 FULL 2 HALF BATHS, 6,712 SQFT

Tuscany Hills is one of Brentwood’s most sought after neighborhoods, and this home backs to protected green space with a walking path to Jordan Elementary. All five bedrooms have their own bath, and the owner’s suite has a soaking tub, an oversized shower and a custom closet with its own laundry. The chef’s kitchen opens to two separate terrace spaces, each with a fireplace, made for slow mornings and long evenings outside. The finished lower level walks out to the yard and adds a home gym, a guest retreat and a theater room with tiered seating. Zoned for Ravenwood High, with community trails and a pool just down the street. - $2,999,900

3 BEDS / 2 FULL BATHS, 2,951 SQFT ON 5.53 ACRES

Five and a half wooded acres on a private, newly repaved lane that is expected to become gated, with I-40 and One Bellevue Place only minutes away. There are two bedrooms plus a large loft, and the seller is willing to enclose the loft as a true third bedroom. A four bay garage gives you room for cars, toys or a workshop. Now under $2 million, it’s a rare chance at real acreage and privacy this close to Nashville. - $1,999,900
