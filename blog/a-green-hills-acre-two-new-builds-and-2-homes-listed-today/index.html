A Green Hills acre, two new builds, and 2 homes listed today

By Jason Kloess · October 30, 2026

Five Fresh Homes Worth Knowing About

Happy Friday there,

Let's jump right in. Williamson County’s 5.3 months of supply and Brentwood’s 61-day average DOM confirm we’re in a buyer-advantaged market for the luxury tier. Inventory is up 15% year-over-year, and sellers need precision, not optimism. Franklin and Brentwood are still moving (97% list-to-sale ratios), but only for homes priced accurately from day one.

The ultra-luxury tier ($4M+) operates on its own clock; 128 average days on market region-wide, but the Compass Coming Soon pipeline this week shows that the best assets are still creating private demand before they ever meet the public market.

Three of this week's five homes are Compass Coming Soon, and the other two hit the market today, so you are seeing every one of them before the weekend crowd does. The lineup runs from an acre between Belle Meade and Forest Hills to brand new builds in Green Hills and Brentwood, a renovated Richland home with its own guest cottage, and a Franklin home that backs to 100 wooded acres, priced from $1.7M to $4.8M. If one of these is the one, reply now and let's get you in first.

Have a wonderful Halloween weekend and we hope to see you soon!

COMING SOON | 4505 BEACON DR, NASHVILLE, TN 37215

5 BEDS / 6 BATHS, 6,393 SQFT, 1.03 ACRES

Meticulously kept by its original owners since it was built in 2017, this home sits on just over an acre between Belle Meade and Forest Hills. The primary suite and an ensuite guest room are both on the main level, along with a private office with built in bookshelves and a fireplace. Upstairs are three more ensuite bedrooms, a bonus room with a wet bar, and a flex space ready to become a bunk room or theater. The open kitchen, breakfast room and family room are backed by a scullery, with a formal dining room for hosting. Outside you get a pool and spa, an outdoor kitchen and bar, and a screened porch, plus a three car garage and a whole home generator.

$4,800,000

COMING SOON | 4206 A BELMONT PARK TER, NASHVILLE, TN 37215

5 BEDS / 7 BATHS, 5,958 SQFT, 0.55 ACRE

Brand new 2026 construction by Paros Group, set back from the street on more than half an acre in Green Hills. A two story foyer with a curved staircase and walls of glass sets the tone, and both the primary and a guest suite are on the main level. The kitchen is built for serious cooking, with a 12 foot island, a 48 inch Wolf range and a separate prep kitchen. Out back, a screened porch with a fireplace overlooks a private pool. Upstairs are three more ensuite bedrooms, a study, a family room with a wet bar and a roof deck, with a three car garage below.

$3,625,000

COMING SOON | 9501 GRAND HAVEN DR, BRENTWOOD, TN 37027

6 BEDS / 8 BATHS, 6,267 SQFT, 0.56 ACRE

Brand new construction in Brentwood's Raintree Reserve, with completion expected December 1, 2026. Roughly 3,900 square feet live on the main level alone, including two bedrooms, a formal office, a separate dining room and an oversized breakfast room under 12 foot ceilings. Every bedroom has its own ensuite bath, and the primary suite adds two large closets and a bath with a separate makeup vanity. Two laundry rooms, a very large pantry, a drop zone and a four car garage show it was planned for busy family life. The yard is flat and irrigated, the neighborhood has a pool, tennis and pickleball courts and trails, and it is zoned for Crockett, Woodland and Ravenwood.

$3,200,000

JUST LISTED | 3910 VALLEY RD, NASHVILLE, TN 37205

6 BEDS / 7 BATHS, 3,832 SQFT, 0.30 ACRE

Originally built in 1948 and renovated in 2022, this Richland and Cherokee Park home pairs historic bones with a clean contemporary design. The open main living area is filled with natural light, and the designer kitchen features white GE Café appliances and Calacatta countertops. The primary bath is a true spa retreat. Out back, a low maintenance turf yard with a putting green still leaves room for a future pool, and a screened porch and covered patio make entertaining easy. A detached single level guest home adds two bedrooms, two baths and vaulted ceilings for guests or multigenerational living, plus a detached garage and a circular drive.

$2,599,900

JUST LISTED | 1148 STONEBRIDGE PARK DR, FRANKLIN, TN 37069

4 BEDS / 6 BATHS, 4,436 SQFT, 0.31 ACRE

Renovated down to the studs, this Franklin home sits on a cul de sac lot backing to roughly 100 acres of woods. The main level has European white oak floors, custom walnut cabinetry and a chef's kitchen with a large island that flows into the great room with a fireplace, the dining area and a screened deck. The owner's suite is on the main level with a spa style bath. Upstairs, three more bedrooms each have a newly renovated bath and a walk in closet. The finished walkout basement adds a large recreation room that opens to a covered patio, with a three car garage and Williamson County schools.

$1,699,900
