Brentwood vs. Franklin

Brentwood is the more expensive city on a typical home: a median sale price of $1.42 million at $356 a square foot, against $863,000 and $317 in Franklin. Brentwood is closer to Nashville (about 20 to 25 minutes to downtown) with consistent one-acre lots. Franklin is bigger and more varied, with a historic Main Street, master-planned communities and the region's largest estates, and its top end goes higher. Both are Williamson County Schools, and property tax is close: about $11,175 on a $3 million home in Brentwood vs $12,150 in Franklin.

  • Brentwood median: $1.42M; Franklin: $863K (3 months to Aug 2026)
  • Franklin homes sold faster: 49 median days vs 63 in Brentwood
  • Brentwood is about 10 minutes closer to downtown Nashville and BNA
  • Franklin's estates go higher: September 2026's top regional sale was $6.72M on Old Hillsboro Road

The difference

Two kinds of Williamson County

Brentwood is consistency. Most of the city has kept a one-acre minimum lot for single-family homes since the 1980s, so established neighborhoods such as Concord Hunt, Witherspoon, River Oaks and Maryland Manor feel leafy and low-density. Above $3 million the market concentrates in Governors Club, Annandale and custom estates on the western and southern edges.

Franklin is several markets in one. Central Franklin around the historic Main Street runs around $1 million; Westhaven, the walkable master-planned community west of downtown, runs from the high $700s to $4 million and up; and the horse properties along Old Hillsboro Road and toward Leiper's Fork regularly trade above $5 million.

Choosing

Which one fits you

Choose Brentwood if commute time to Nashville matters, you want an acre in an established neighborhood, or you are looking at the gated golf and estate communities. It held firmer than the wider region in 2026.

Choose Franklin if you want a real town center, a walkable master-planned community, acreage, or the very top of the estate market. It was the tightest market in the areas we track this year, with homes closing at 98.3% of list.

Check the school district on every Franklin address: parts of the city are zoned for the Franklin Special School District for kindergarten through eighth grade, then Williamson County high schools.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Brentwood or Franklin more expensive?
Brentwood, on a typical home. Its median sale price was $1,424,058 ($356 a square foot) over the three months to August 2026, against $862,929 ($317) in Franklin (Redfin). Franklin's highest-priced estates sell for more than Brentwood's, though.
Which has lower property taxes, Brentwood or Franklin?
Brentwood, slightly. At 2026 rates the bill on a $3 million home is about $11,175 in Brentwood ($1.49 per $100 of assessed value) and about $12,150 in Franklin ($1.62). Both are roughly half the Davidson County bill.
Are Brentwood and Franklin in the same school district?
Both are served by Williamson County Schools. Parts of Franklin are zoned for the Franklin Special School District for kindergarten through eighth grade. Confirm by address.
How far are Brentwood and Franklin from downtown Nashville?
About 20 to 25 minutes from Brentwood and 30 to 35 from Franklin, off-peak. Add 10 to 15 minutes at rush hour on I-65.
Where are the gated communities in Brentwood and Franklin?
In Brentwood: Governors Club and Annandale. In Franklin: Laurelbrooke and Legends Ridge. See the neighborhood guides for each.
