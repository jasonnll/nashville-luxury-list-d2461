Governors Club vs. Annandale

Both are gated Brentwood estate communities, but they suit different buyers. Governors Club is the larger, golf-centered one: about 438 homesites on 618 acres off Concord Road, a staffed 24/7 gate and a private Arnold Palmer course (membership optional), with homes mostly built from the late 1990s. Annandale is newer and smaller: about 180 homes on 221 acres, built from 2006, with pools, a 14-acre park and trails instead of golf. One detail decides a lot in Annandale: a small part of it is in Davidson County, so taxes and schools must be checked by parcel.

  • Governors Club: ~438 homesites, staffed gate, Arnold Palmer course, sales about $1.35M–$7M over 12 months to Oct 2026
  • Annandale: ~180 homes built since 2006, two pools, cabana, 14-acre park and trails
  • Governors Club is entirely Williamson County; Annandale is mostly Williamson with a small Davidson portion
  • Club membership at Governors Club is a separate, optional decision

Choosing

Which one fits you

Choose Governors Club if golf or a staffed gate is part of the appeal, or if you want the widest range of homes inside one gate, from older homes needing updates around $1.35 million to renovated estates on the course near $7 million. Homes on the fairways or with long views carry a clear premium. Buying does not require joining the club.

Choose Annandale if you want newer construction without a golf club: open plans, large kitchens, finished lower levels and three-car garages that buyers from older Brentwood neighborhoods often end up renovating toward. Check which county each home is in before you compare prices: a Davidson parcel carries Nashville's tax rate and Metro schools.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you have to join the club to live in Governors Club?
No. Club membership is a separate, optional decision with several tiers, including social memberships.
Is Annandale in Williamson County?
Mostly. A small portion of Annandale is in Davidson County, which changes the property tax rate and the public schools, so check each home by parcel rather than by its Brentwood mailing address.
Which is newer, Governors Club or Annandale?
Annandale. Construction began in 2006. Most Governors Club homes were built from the late 1990s on.
What schools serve Governors Club?
Crockett Elementary, Woodland Middle and Ravenwood High in Williamson County Schools. Confirm by address.
