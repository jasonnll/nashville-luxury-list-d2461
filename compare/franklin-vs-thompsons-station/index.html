Franklin vs. Thompson's Station

Thompson's Station is the value play next door to Franklin: a median sale price of $829,451 at $302 a square foot, against $862,929 and $317 in Franklin over the same three months to August 2026. You generally get more house and land for the money in Thompson's Station, in master-planned communities such as Tollgate Village, Bridgemore Village and Canterbury. Franklin offers the historic Main Street, more established neighborhoods and a much deeper estate market. Both are Williamson County Schools.

  • Thompson's Station: $302 a square foot vs $317 in Franklin
  • Franklin homes sold faster: 49 median days vs 64
  • Thompson's Station homes sold closer to list: 99.2% vs 98.3%

Choosing

Which one fits you

Choose Thompson's Station if you want newer construction, a larger home for the budget and a quieter setting, and don't need to be in downtown Franklin every day. Thompson's Station Elementary holds a 9/10 GreatSchools rating.

Choose Franklin if you want a walkable town center, a wider choice of neighborhoods, or the top of the market: Franklin's estates along Old Hillsboro Road regularly trade above $5 million.
