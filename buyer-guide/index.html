Step-by-step process

Your roadmap to buying Nashville luxury

01

Define Your Vision

Before we start, we align on your must-haves, ideal neighborhoods, lifestyle priorities, and timeline. This isn't a checklist conversation; it's a deep-dive into what makes the right home for you. We'll discuss off-market possibilities from day one.

Neighborhood priorities · Must-have features · Timeline & flexibility · Off-market opportunities

02

Access Off-Market Properties

The best Nashville luxury homes often sell before they're ever listed. Through our network of 300+ agents and private seller relationships, you'll see exclusive opportunities weeks, sometimes months, before the public. We send you a curated list weekly.

Private listings · Agent network access · Weekly curated digest · Pre-market exclusives

03

Private Showings & Deep Dives

Every showing is private: no open houses, no competing buyers looking over your shoulder. We evaluate each property together, discussing construction quality, lot positioning, future value, and any red flags. For relocation clients, we offer immersive virtual tours.

Private showings · Virtual tours · Construction review · Value analysis

04

Crafting the Winning Offer

In Nashville's luxury market, price isn't always the deciding factor. We craft offers that appeal to seller priorities (terms, contingencies, closing timeline, and personal touch) while protecting your interests and your earnest money.

Strategic offer terms · Contingency strategy · Seller motivation · Earnest money protection

05

Due Diligence & Inspection

We coordinate every inspection (structural, mechanical, foundation, pool, and specialty systems) with vetted luxury home inspectors who know what to look for in high-end properties. We review reports together and negotiate repairs or credits as needed.

Luxury inspectors · Specialty reviews · Repair negotiation · Title & survey

06

Seamless Closing & Beyond

From coordinating attorneys and title companies to scheduling movers and connecting you with local service providers, we manage every detail through closing day and beyond. Our relationship doesn't end at the closing table.

Closing coordination · Attorney & title · Mover referrals · Ongoing support

Buyer FAQ

Common buyer questions

Do buyers pay a commission?
Buyer representation is set out in a written agreement you sign before touring, as required since the 2024 NAR settlement. The fee is negotiable and is often paid by the seller as a negotiated term, but that is not guaranteed. Jason walks you through the options on your first call.
How do I see off-market listings?
Off-market properties are shared through two channels: (1) our weekly VIP email digest sent to 600+ subscribers, and (2) direct outreach from Jason when a property matches your specific criteria. The best way to access them is to connect with Jason directly.
Can you help me relocate from out of state?
Absolutely. A significant portion of our buyers relocate from New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, and other major metros. We offer virtual walkthroughs, neighborhood video tours, and a full concierge experience, including connections to attorneys, mortgage brokers, and moving companies. Start with the Nashville Relocation Guide.
How competitive is the Nashville luxury market?
Across Davidson and Williamson counties, the typical $1.5M+ home that sold in the last six months went under contract in 40 days and closed at 98.3% of its list price (Realtracs MLS, October 2026). The best-priced homes still move quickly, while homes above $3M take longer and leave more room to negotiate. Having an agent with off-market access and strong seller relationships is the single biggest advantage you can have.
What should I have ready before making an offer?
At minimum, a pre-approval letter from a lender familiar with jumbo loans (or proof of funds for cash buyers). For luxury transactions, we recommend having your financial documentation ready to move fast when the right property appears. The mortgage calculator shows payments at 2026 Tennessee tax rates.
