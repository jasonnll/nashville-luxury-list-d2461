Nashville

The East Bank finally gets its moment: a new stadium, Oracle's headquarters and a new street grid, plus new ways in and out of the city and a wave of branded residences.

01

The Big Picture

Stadium, riverfront, airport and transit.

Opening 2027East Bank

New Nissan Stadium

Construction wraps Feb. 2027 · Events from April · Titans' 2027 season

An enclosed, roughly 60,000-seat stadium under a translucent roof, built next to the current one, which comes down afterward to make room for more development. Because it's climate-controlled, the city can book major events year-round.

  • About $2.2 billion; Titans, state and revenue bonds share the cost
  • Seats roughly a third closer to the field than the old stadium
  • Satellite Robert's Western World and a Bluebird Cafe partnership inside
  • Host of Super Bowl LXIV in 2030

What to watch: how a full year of big events changes demand for downtown, Germantown and East Bank condos and short-term stays.

Under WayEast Bank · River North

Oracle's Global HQ & the East Bank

Broke ground Nov. 10, 2026 · First buildings targeted for 2030

Oracle has started its $4.5 billion riverfront headquarters, about 80 acres with offices, retail, green space, an amphitheater, a Nobu hotel and a pedestrian bridge to Germantown. It sits inside a 550-acre East Bank plan that Metro is rebuilding from scratch.

  • Up to 8,500 Oracle employees expected by 2031
  • First Nobu restaurant and hotel in Tennessee
  • James Robertson Pkwy closes starting Q2 2027 so it can be lowered to meet the new East Bank Boulevard
  • River North Greenway and Landings at River North already open

What to watch: expect detours on the east side of downtown in 2027, and closer interest in East Nashville, Germantown and McFerrin Park.

Under WayBNA ⇄ Downtown

Music City Loop

Tunneling under way · First segment targeted for around 2027

The Boring Company's privately funded tunnel will carry riders in Tesla vehicles between the airport and downtown. Planned stops include BNA, the Music City Center and the State Capitol, and residents of Giarratana's Church Street towers are getting their own station.

  • The company says the airport-to-downtown ride will take about 8 minutes
  • Roughly 10 miles in the first phase
  • Opening has already slipped from 2026, so treat any date as a target

What to watch: if it opens on time, buildings near a station could become more appealing to frequent flyers.

Under WayNashville International Airport

BNA New Horizon

Central Core done Dec. 2027 · New Concourse A in July 2028

BNA's $3 billion expansion keeps going as passenger counts hit records. In 2027 the work is on the terminal's central core (entrances, elevators, escalators) and a larger central ramp. A rebuilt Concourse A follows in 2028.

  • Built to handle up to 40 million passengers a year
  • Central ramp expansion is due September 2027
  • New Concourse A adds a larger Admirals Club, Eric Church's Chief's and Hawkers

What to watch: allow extra time at BNA through 2027. Easy flights matter a lot to relocation and second-home buyers.

02

The 2027 Stage

Nashville's biggest year of live events in a long time.

2027 EventDowntown + New Nissan Stadium

CMA Fest in the New Stadium

June 10–13, 2027

CMA Fest's nightly stadium shows move into the new, climate-controlled Nissan Stadium. Free daytime stages and fan experiences still spread across downtown.

  • The first CMA Fest in the new venue
  • Four-night stadium passes are already on sale
  • Parking will be very tight, so plan on rideshare or the pedestrian bridge

What to watch: one of the busiest weeks of the year downtown. Avoid scheduling showings or a move that week.

2027 EventNew Nissan Stadium

The Stadium's First Headliners

Summer 2027

The first concerts in the new stadium are on sale. Kenny Chesney, who has played more shows at the old stadium than any other artist, is booked as one of the first headliners.

  • Jul 3: Kenny Chesney with Eric Church, Sheryl Crow & Greylan James
  • Aug 27: Morgan Wallen with HARDY, Cole Swindell & Stella Lefty
  • 2027: Banana Ball Championship Game
  • Fall: The Titans' first home season

What to watch: more announcements are expected. We'll add them here as they're confirmed.

03

Stay & Live

New hotels and branded residences, including some that open in 2028 but are selling now.

Opening 2027Midtown · 1717 Hayes St

VOCE Hotel & Residences

Slated for November 2027

A 25-story tower near Vanderbilt and West End that combines a boutique hotel with privately owned residences. Owners can opt into a hotel-managed rental program. It's billed as Nashville's first condo-hotel.

  • 192 residences, from studios to penthouses, plus 114 hotel suites
  • Rooftop dining and pool, spa, members' speakeasy
  • About 60,000 sq ft of boutique office space

What to watch: a new kind of ownership for Nashville. Read the rental-program terms and HOA documents closely before you buy.

Opening 2027Downtown & West End

New Stays & Refreshes

Through 2027

New downtown hotels and a long-standing West End hotel's renovation, coming after a 2026 that saw Dolly Parton's Songteller Hotel open.

  • Jan 2027: final phase of Loews Nashville at Vanderbilt Plaza's renovation
  • Dec 2027: Moxy Downtown Capitol District, 185 rooms
  • Dec 2027 (target): boutique hotel in the historic Morris Building on Charlotte Ave

What to watch: more hotel rooms tend to bring more restaurants and nightlife nearby.

Looking to 2028The Gulch · Paseo South Gulch

Pendry Nashville & Residences

Tower completes late 2027 · Hotel opens early 2028

A 30-story tower with a Pendry hotel on the lower floors and 146 branded residences above. Residents share the hotel's restaurants and also get a private rooftop pool, dining room and a whiskey and billiards room.

  • 180 hotel rooms and suites
  • One- to four-bedroom residences, up to about 3,800 sq ft
  • Neighbors include Prima, Equinox, Catbird Seat and Patterson House

What to watch: 2027 is the pre-construction buying window for the best floor plans.

Looking to 2028Belle Meade · Harding Pike

1 Iris Lane at Belle Meade Village

Under construction · Completion targeted for 2028

The old Belle Meade Plaza site is becoming a walkable village around a town square with an antique carousel. The plan includes two boutique condo buildings, a members' club, a boutique hotel and more than 40 storefronts.

  • 92 condos, reported starting around $3M
  • Wellness amenities include Pilates, spa, cold plunge and a salon
  • About 6 acres of green space and access to the Richland Creek greenway

What to watch: one of the few new lock-and-leave options right next to Belle Meade.

Looking to 2028Downtown · 1010 Church St

Paramount

Substantial completion targeted for May 2028

Giarratana's 60-story, roughly 750-foot tower will be the tallest building in Tennessee. It has apartments and for-sale condos above a new addition to the downtown YMCA, and it's across from the Nashville Public Library.

  • 140 condos and 360 apartments
  • Linked to a planned Music City Loop station for Giarratana's Church Street towers

What to watch: the condos with the best views tend to sell well before the tower is finished.

Looking to 2028Pie Town

The Hoxton, Nashville

Now listed for March 2028

Ennismore's Hoxton is coming to Pie Town, in a building with arched brick and a rooftop pool. Plans include a speakeasy downstairs and private dining rooms built around a shared kitchen.

  • About 200 rooms
  • Joins Hoxtons in New York, Chicago, LA and Portland

What to watch: Pie Town and SoBro keep getting more walkable.

Franklin

A new civic heart on the Public Square, Pilgrimage's return in the spring, and the most private-club and estate-lot activity Williamson County has seen in years.

01

The Big Picture

Civic projects, parks and the festival.

Opening 2027Historic Public Square

New Franklin City Hall

Topped out June 2026 · Opening targeted for September 2027

The 1970s mall building that housed City Hall is gone. In its place is a three-story civic building designed to fit downtown's historic character, with public space around it.

  • About 200 new underground parking spaces downtown
  • Larger plaza on the Square plus a one-acre public park
  • Ground-floor commercial space and new public restrooms

What to watch: extra parking and a park could help downtown and the historic streets around it, like Hincheyville and Third Ave.

2027 EventThe Park at Harlinsdale Farm

Pilgrimage Festival Returns

April 10–11, 2027

After skipping 2026, Franklin's music and culture festival comes back for its 12th year and moves from September to spring.

  • Headliners: Post Malone and Megan Moroney
  • Also on the lineup: Mt. Joy, Better Than Ezra and Moon Taxi
  • Past years have drawn up to about 30,000 people a day

What to watch: expect a busy weekend near Franklin Rd and Mack Hatcher. Schedule open houses around it.

Under WayCarothers Pkwy · Southeast Franklin

The Pearl (Pearlene M. Bransford Park)

Broke ground March 2025 · Opening around 2028

A city park of about 180 acres between Carothers Parkway and I-65, named for longtime public servant Pearlene M. Bransford.

  • Ellie G's Dream World, an inclusive playground
  • Football and lacrosse fields, plus an accessible riverwalk on the Harpeth
  • Robinson Lake with a fishing dock; a new sidewalk will link it to Ladd Park

What to watch: a big new amenity for Ladd Park, Berry Farms and the surrounding neighborhoods.

ProposedHillsboro Rd · Brownland Farm

The Banks at Brownland

Going through city and county review

BLVD Capital wants to turn 233-acre Brownland Farm, just outside downtown, into a mixed-use youth sports complex. The centerpiece would be an ice facility run by the Nashville Predators.

  • About 100,000 sq ft of rink space, owned by the city and county and run by the Predators
  • Would be Williamson County's first public facility of its kind
  • Some neighbors have raised concerns about traffic and the farm's future

What to watch: approval votes in 2027 could change the Hillsboro Rd corridor.

02

New in Town

Shopping, dining and a boutique hotel.

Opening 2027Columbia Ave at Margin St

The Margin District

Delivering in 2027

A $165 million mixed-use project at the edge of the downtown historic district, about a block south of Five Points. It adds walkable shopping, offices and a small set of luxury condos.

  • About 180,000 sq ft in total
  • 50,000 sq ft of street-level retail and dining, plus 50,000 sq ft of office
  • 25 residences, roughly $2M–$6.7M, with private terraces and gated parking

What to watch: one of the very few ways to own a new, lock-and-leave home you can walk from to Main Street.

Looking to 2028The Factory at Franklin

The Factory Hotel

Breaks ground late 2026 · Opens spring 2028

The Factory at Franklin, a renovated historic stove works, is adding a 120-room boutique hotel in Marriott's Tribute Portfolio. Plans also include a full-service restaurant and bar, plus more parking.

  • Design draws on the Factory's industrial history
  • Expanded parking for the whole Factory campus

What to watch: a stylish place for visiting family to stay, close to downtown.

Looking to 2028CoolSprings Galleria

DICK'S House of Sport

Build-out under way · Opening early 2028

The first House of Sport in the Nashville area will take over the former JCPenney space at CoolSprings Galleria.

  • More than 100,000 sq ft
  • Climbing wall, TrackMan golf simulators and multi-sport batting cages

What to watch: part of CoolSprings Galleria's shift toward experiences and mixed use.

03

Clubs, Communities & Estates

Golf, private clubs, a new village and large-acreage homesites.

Club Under WaySpring Hill (Maury Co.)

Firefly

Golf opened Labor Day 2026 · Clubhouse and amenities under construction

A private, gated golf community of more than 700 acres about 30 miles south of Nashville. Its 18-hole course and 9-hole short course are Andrew Green's first original designs. He's best known for restoring Oak Hill, East Lake and Scioto.

  • More than 80 homesites sold in the first 45 days
  • Racquet club with tennis, padel and indoor pickleball, plus a family club and bowling
  • Spring Hill address, in Maury County, right at the Williamson County line

What to watch: 2027 is when homes start going up and the club begins to feel like a neighborhood.

Under WayWest Harpeth River · Del Rio Pike

Wyelea

Homesites closing · Members' club being built

Just 68 homesites on about 600 acres along the West Harpeth River, with a private members' club run by Daniel Communities, the developer behind Reynolds Lake Oconee and High Hampton.

  • Homesites from under an acre to 10 acres, starting around $1.5M
  • Sewer is already in, so there's no long septic-approval wait
  • Equestrian facilities, a lake for paddling and access to the river

What to watch: Daniel's club amenities will set the tone for the whole community.

ApprovedHillview Ln · near Winstead Hill

Harlin

Approved by Franklin's aldermen · Phased build-out

Boyle Investment Company's 311-acre walkable village at Franklin's southern edge. It's the first project under the city's "Village Green" rules, which require about 70% open space.

  • More than 200 homes
  • Boutique hotel and village shops
  • Some nearby residents opposed it over traffic

What to watch: a new kind of walkable neighborhood for South Franklin.

First Homes RisingLeiper's Fork · Hargrove Rd

The Arbors at Leiper's Fork

Gate finished · First homes under construction

A private, gated community on about 380 acres of hardwood forest a few minutes from Leiper's Fork village, with a small number of very large homesites.

  • Homesites of 5 to 29 acres, about $1M–$3M
  • More than 5 miles of trails, plus tennis
  • About 40% of homesites sold within three months of launch

What to watch: before buying acreage, confirm design review rules, septic and build timelines.
