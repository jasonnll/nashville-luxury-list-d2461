Our proven process

6-step luxury marketing system

01

Precision pricing strategy

Every dollar of list price matters. We analyze private sales, off-market comparables, and active buyer demand to identify the exact price range that maximizes your net proceeds while attracting serious, qualified buyers. Overpricing in luxury is the fastest way to leave money on the table.

Private sale comps · Absorption rate analysis · Buyer pool sizing · Net proceeds modeling

02

Pre-market preparation

We connect you with our vetted vendors (stagers, landscapers, handymen and cleaning crews) to ensure your property makes a flawless first impression. For larger renovations, we provide guidance on what creates value and what doesn't in the Nashville luxury market.

Luxury staging · Landscaping · Pre-list improvements · ROI guidance

03

World-class photography & video

Your listing is a buyer's first impression, and it needs to stop the scroll. We commission Nashville's top architectural photographers, produce cinematic video tours, commission aerial drone footage, and create interactive 3D walkthroughs that bring the property to life for remote buyers.

Architectural photography · Cinematic video · Drone & aerial · 3D Matterport tours

04

Pre-market exposure

Before we go public, we run a targeted pre-market campaign: alerting our network of 600+ VIP buyers, notifying relocating executives, and providing exclusivity to serious buyers who prefer discretion. This pre-market phase frequently results in off-market offers above asking.

VIP buyer network · Relocation buyers · Private showings · Off-market offers

05

Global marketing campaign

Through Compass's global platform, your listing reaches qualified buyers in 70+ countries. We deploy targeted digital advertising campaigns on Meta and Google, featured placement on all major portals (Zillow, Realtor.com, Compass.com), and direct outreach to international buyer networks.

70+ country reach · Digital advertising · All major portals · International networks

06

Negotiation, contracts & closing

Jason personally handles every negotiation: reviewing offers, countering strategically, and protecting your interests through inspection, appraisal, and financing contingencies. Our 96% list-to-sale ratio reflects offers we turn into closed transactions at full value.

Offer review & strategy · Counter-offer expertise · Inspection negotiation · Closing management

Seller FAQ

Common seller questions

Can we sell without listing publicly?
Yes. For many luxury sellers, privacy is paramount. We can run a targeted private campaign through our VIP buyer network before, or instead of, any public listing. Some of our best results come from off-market transactions where both parties prefer discretion.
How do you determine the list price?
We build a Comparative Market Analysis using both MLS sales and off-market transactions, then layer in current buyer demand, days-on-market trends, and your specific property's unique attributes. We never guess; we show you the data.
How long does it take to sell a luxury home in Nashville?
Our average days on market is 18, well below the Nashville luxury market average. Correctly priced, beautifully presented homes with strong marketing consistently sell faster and for more money than properties that sit and go stale.
What is your commission structure?
We discuss commission during our initial consultation. For luxury listings, we offer competitive, transparent fee structures that reflect our comprehensive marketing investment. We're happy to walk through exactly what's included and how our approach compares.
Should I renovate before selling?
It depends entirely on the property and the buyer pool. In many cases, targeted improvements (kitchen updates, exterior refresh, staging) can meaningfully increase your sale price. In others, selling as-is to a buyer who wants to customize is the smarter play. We give you honest, data-backed guidance.
