How to Buy a Luxury Home in Nashville

Buying a luxury home in Nashville takes seven steps: a strategy call, financing or proof of funds, an on- and off-market search, tours, an offer, due diligence and closing, usually 30 to 45 days after contract. In Tennessee the buyer customarily pays the $0.37 per $100 transfer tax.

Step by step

The seven steps

01

Strategy call

Thirty minutes on timing, budget, must-haves and the neighborhoods that fit. You leave with a short list of areas and a plan for the search, including what to watch off-market.

02

Financing or proof of funds

Get pre-approved with a lender who does jumbo loans; in 2026 any loan over $832,750 is jumbo in Davidson and Williamson counties. Cash buyers need a recent proof-of-funds letter. Sellers at this level expect one or the other with every offer.

03

Search, on and off the market

Jason sets up an MLS search and adds you to the weekly list of Private Exclusives and Coming Soon homes, then calls agents about homes that fit before they list.

04

Tours, in person or virtual

Tour in person, or by live video if you're relocating. Jason checks each home's county line, school zone, HOA rules and flood zone before you fall for it.

05

Offer and negotiation

Tennessee uses a standard purchase and sale agreement with an earnest money deposit, an inspection period and a closing date. Price is one lever; inspection terms, closing date and seller credits are others.

06

Due diligence

Inspection, plus specialists as needed: roof, HVAC, pool, sewer scope, termite, structural. Appraisal, survey and title follow. For acreage: easements, septic, well and greenbelt status.

07

Closing

Most Tennessee closings happen at a title company about 30 to 45 days after contract, or faster with cash. Buyers customarily pay the state transfer tax of $0.37 per $100 of price, plus mortgage tax of $0.115 per $100 of the loan above $2,000.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does it take to buy a luxury home in Nashville?
From contract to closing usually takes 30 to 45 days with a loan, sometimes less with cash. The search itself depends on inventory and how specific you are; buyers who join the off-market list often see the right home sooner.
Who pays the transfer tax in Tennessee?
The state realty transfer tax is $0.37 per $100 of the purchase price, and by custom the buyer pays it, though it's negotiable. On a $2 million home that's $7,400. Buyers with a loan also pay mortgage tax of $0.115 per $100 of the loan above $2,000.
What is a jumbo loan in Nashville?
In 2026 a jumbo loan is any loan over $832,750, the conforming limit for Davidson and Williamson counties. Jumbo loans usually need a larger down payment and more cash reserves.
Can I buy a home in Nashville before I move?
Yes. Many of Jason's clients buy from out of state with live video tours, a trusted local inspector and remote closing options.
