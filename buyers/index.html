FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I buy a luxury home in Nashville?
Start with a strategy call, get pre-approved for a jumbo loan or gather proof of funds, join the off-market list, tour (in person or by video), make an offer under Tennessee's standard purchase agreement, complete due diligence and close at a title company, usually 30 to 45 days after contract. See the full buying process.
Is it a buyer's market for luxury homes in Nashville?
The $1M+ tier leans toward buyers. In September 2026 there were about 2,289 $1M+ homes for sale against roughly 460 closings a month, about five months of supply, and 43% of active $1M+ listings were below their original asking price. See the monthly market report for the latest.
How do I find off-market homes in Nashville?
Join the weekly Nashville Luxury List at /newsletter/. It includes Compass Private Exclusives and Coming Soon homes plus homes Jason hears about from other agents before they're public.
What does a buyer pay at closing in Tennessee?
By custom the buyer pays the state transfer tax of $0.37 per $100 of price, plus mortgage tax of $0.115 per $100 of the loan above $2,000, title insurance and lender fees. The mortgage calculator estimates the taxes.
Which Nashville neighborhoods are best for luxury buyers?
Belle Meade, Green Hills, Forest Hills and Oak Hill in Davidson County; Brentwood, Franklin and College Grove in Williamson County, plus gated and golf communities such as Governors Club, Laurelbrooke and The Grove. The neighborhood guides compare them.
