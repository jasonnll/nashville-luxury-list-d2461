FAQ

Questions, answered

Does it cost anything to work with a buyer's agent?
Since August 2024 you'll sign a written buyer agreement before touring that states how your agent is paid. Often the seller offers to cover it, but not always. Jason explains the options up front.
Can I see homes that aren't on Zillow?
Yes. Compass Private Exclusives and Coming Soon homes, plus homes Jason hears about from other agents, go to the weekly list before most reach the public sites.
Do you help with new construction?
Yes, including negotiating with builders. One relocating client came away with an extra $20,000 toward closing costs.
