FAQ

Searching for a home, answered

Does this search show every home for sale?
It shows current $1M+ listings from Realtracs, the Middle Tennessee MLS, newest first. Not every home for sale appears, and homes that are coming soon or selling privately never reach the MLS. Use the form on this page and Jason will send the full list for your search, including those.
Which areas can I search?
All of Nashville and Williamson County, or one area at a time: Belle Meade, Green Hills, Forest Hills & Oak Hill, 12 South, East Nashville and Sylvan Park in Davidson County, and Brentwood, Franklin, Governors Club, Annandale, Laurelbrooke, Westhaven, Leiper's Fork, Legends Ridge, College Grove, The Grove and Troubadour in Williamson County.
Will I leave this site when I open a listing?
Each home opens in a new tab, so your search stays right here.
How do I see homes before they hit the market?
Join Off-Market Access for Jason's weekly list of private and coming-soon homes, or ask him directly at 615-457-0768.
