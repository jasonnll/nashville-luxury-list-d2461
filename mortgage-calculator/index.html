FAQ

Questions, answered

How is property tax calculated in Tennessee?
Homes are assessed at 25% of appraised value, and the local rate is applied per $100 of assessed value. In 2026 that's $1.49 in Brentwood, $1.62 in Franklin, $1.30 in unincorporated Williamson County, and $2.814 in Nashville's Urban Services District.
What is the jumbo loan limit in Nashville?
For 2026, loans above $832,750 are jumbo in Davidson and Williamson counties.
What taxes do buyers pay at closing in Tennessee?
The realty transfer tax of $0.37 per $100 of price (customarily paid by the buyer) and, with a loan, mortgage tax of $0.115 per $100 of the loan above $2,000.
