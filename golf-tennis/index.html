Nashville Golf & Tennis Guide

Around Nashville you can play a Jack Nicklaus, Arnold Palmer, Greg Norman or Tom Fazio course at a private club, or tee off at one of seven Metro Parks courses inside the city. This guide covers both, plus where to play tennis and which neighborhoods sit closest.

  • 13 private clubs from Belle Meade to College Grove
  • Seven Metro Parks courses, plus daily-fee Gaylord Springs, Hermitage and Franklin Bridge
  • Public tennis at the Centennial Sportsplex: 12 outdoor and 4 indoor courts
  • Neighborhoods matched to the courses

Tennis & pickleball

Where to play tennis

Public

  • Centennial Sportsplex Tennis Center. 224 25th Ave N, near Centennial Park. 12 outdoor and 4 indoor hard courts, plus youth and pickleball courts. Published rates about $10/hour outdoors, $25/hour indoors.
  • Neighborhood parks. Metro Parks and the cities of Brentwood and Franklin maintain free courts in many parks; check each city's parks site.
  • Pickleball. Growing fast at public parks and most clubs.

Private

  • Country clubs. Belle Meade, Richland, Hillwood, Nashville Golf & Athletic, Brentwood CC, Old Natchez and Temple Hills all have tennis.
  • Community amenities. Laurelbrooke, Legends Ridge, The Grove and many HOAs include courts for residents.
  • Ask about. Leagues, junior programs, pros on staff and court reservations.

FAQ

Golf and tennis questions, answered

What are the best public golf courses in Nashville?
Harpeth Hills in Percy Warner Park is the most popular Metro Parks course. Gaylord Springs, the 36 holes at Hermitage Golf Course and Franklin Bridge Golf Club (formerly The Crossing) are the main daily-fee options.
Is there a public golf course inside the city?
Yes. McCabe in Sylvan Park has 27 holes, and Shelby, Ted Rhodes, Two Rivers and Percy Warner are all Metro Parks courses within Nashville.
Where can I play public tennis in Nashville?
The Centennial Sportsplex Tennis Center at 224 25th Ave N has 12 outdoor and 4 indoor hard courts, with published rates of about $10 an hour outdoors and $25 an hour indoors. Many parks also have free courts, and most private clubs have tennis programs.
Which neighborhoods are best for golfers?
Governors Club, Westhaven, The Grove and Troubadour have private courses inside the community. Legends Ridge sits next to Vanderbilt Legends Club, and Belle Meade and Sylvan Park are minutes from Belle Meade CC, Harpeth Hills and McCabe.
Which clubs are best for tennis?
Most private clubs have tennis; Nashville Golf & Athletic Club, Richland, Belle Meade and Temple Hills are known for active programs. Ask each club about leagues, pickleball and junior programs.
