Questions, answered

What is Nashville Luxury List?
Nashville Luxury List is the luxury real estate brand of Jason Kloess, a REALTOR® with Compass in Nashville. It covers $1.5M+ homes, estates and off-market listings across Davidson and Williamson counties, with a weekly list of the best homes, neighborhood guides and a monthly market report.
How do I see off-market homes in Nashville?
Join the weekly list at /newsletter/. It includes Compass Private Exclusives and Coming Soon homes that aren't on Zillow or the MLS yet, plus homes Jason hears about through his agent network.
What areas do you cover?
Davidson County (Belle Meade, Green Hills, Forest Hills, Oak Hill, 12 South, East Nashville and more) and Williamson County (Brentwood, Franklin, College Grove, Leiper's Fork and the gated and golf communities).
Do you only work with $1.5M+ homes?
That is the focus, and every guide and report is written for that buyer and seller. Jason is glad to help with a move at any price point, especially when it leads to the next luxury home.
