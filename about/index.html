Founder

Jason Kloess

REALTOR®, CLHMS · Million Dollar GUILD™ · Compass Tennessee, LLC · License #357138

With 7+ years specializing in Nashville's luxury market, Jason Kloess brings unparalleled access to the city's most coveted properties — many of which never appear on public portals. His boutique approach means every client receives direct, personal attention from contract through closing.

Backed by Compass's global platform and a network built over 47+ transactions, Jason combines hyper-local intelligence with world-class marketing to consistently deliver results for buyers and sellers in Nashville's $1.5M+ segment.

As a Nashville resident himself, Jason understands the nuances that make each luxury enclave unique — from the private gated estates of Belle Meade to the vibrant walkability of Green Hills, to the award-winning schools and family communities of Brentwood and Franklin.

Jason holds the Certified Luxury Home Marketing Specialist (CLHMS) designation and is a member of the Million Dollar GUILD™, both awarded by The Institute for Luxury Home Marketing — the industry's leading authority on training and recognizing agents in the upper-tier residential market.
