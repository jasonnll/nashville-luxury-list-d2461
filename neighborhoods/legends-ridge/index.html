Overview

Living in Legends Ridge

Legends Ridge sits on the ridges of north Franklin, near Hillsboro Road, beside Vanderbilt Legends Club. It is smaller and quieter than Laurelbrooke, with custom homes on lots of about an acre and a handful of larger tracts of five to seven acres.

The entrance is gated but not staffed. Shared amenities include a pool, a clubhouse, tennis and a lake. Many homes take advantage of the topography with walk-out lower levels and long views to the west.

Vanderbilt Legends Club next door is a private 36-hole club and home of Vanderbilt's golf teams. Membership is separate from Legends Ridge, but living next door makes the club an easy choice for golfers.

The market

Home prices in Legends Ridge

Over the 12 months to October 2026, Legends Ridge sales ran from about $1.6M to $5.5M. Larger tracts, newer or renovated homes and homes with views command the top of the range.

Legends Ridge is often a good value alternative to Laurelbrooke for buyers who want a gate and an acre but don't need a staffed gatehouse.

FAQ

Legends Ridge questions, answered

Is Legends Ridge guard-gated?
Legends Ridge has a gated entrance, but there is no staffed gatehouse.
What are HOA fees in Legends Ridge?
Annual dues have run about $1,780 to $3,228. Confirm current dues in the HOA documents.
Does living in Legends Ridge include a Vanderbilt Legends Club membership?
No. Vanderbilt Legends Club is a private club with its own separate membership.
What schools serve Legends Ridge?
Legends Ridge is currently zoned to Walnut Grove Elementary, Grassland Middle and Franklin High. Confirm the assignment for a specific address with the district.
