Overview

Living in Governors Club

Governors Club is the answer most Brentwood locals give when someone asks where the gated golf community is. The neighborhood sits on about 618 acres of rolling land off Concord Road in east Brentwood, with roughly 438 homesites wrapped around a private Arnold Palmer-designed 18-hole course. A staffed gate controls the single main entrance around the clock.

The homes are custom estates, most built from the late 1990s on, in traditional Southern, European and transitional styles. Lots range from about half an acre to more than two acres, and many back to the fairways or to wooded ridgelines. The historic Pleasant Hill mansion, built in 1854, sits within the community and gives it a sense of age that newer gated developments don't have.

Golf is part of the identity, but buying a home here does not require joining the club. Membership is a separate decision with its own tiers, including social options, so buyers who don't play can still live inside the gate without paying golf dues.

The market

Home prices in Governors Club

Over the 12 months to October 2026, Governors Club sales ran from about $1.35M for older homes needing updates to roughly $7M for renovated estates on the course. Homes on the fairways or with long views trade at a clear premium to interior lots, and fully updated homes move fastest.

Because resale inventory inside the gate is thin, a fair share of trades happen quietly before or without broad marketing. If you want to be here, the practical step is to set up a search with an agent who hears about homes before they list.

FAQ

Governors Club questions, answered

Is Governors Club gated?
Yes. Governors Club has a staffed gatehouse on Concord Road that is manned 24 hours a day.
Do you have to join the club to live in Governors Club?
No. Club membership is separate from home ownership and is optional. The club offers several membership tiers, including social options; confirm current terms and fees directly with the club.
What are HOA fees in Governors Club?
HOA dues have run roughly $250 to $465 a month depending on the section of the community. Confirm the current amount and any transfer fees in the HOA documents before you write an offer.
What schools serve Governors Club?
Governors Club is in Williamson County Schools and is currently zoned to Crockett Elementary, Woodland Middle and Ravenwood High. Zones change as the district grows, so confirm the assignment for a specific address with the district.
How much do homes in Governors Club cost?
Over the 12 months to October 2026, homes sold from about $1.35M to about $7M, with course-front and renovated estates at the top of the range.
