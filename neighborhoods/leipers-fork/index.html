Overview

Living in Leiper's Fork

Leiper's Fork is the place people describe when they say they want to live in the country but stay close to Franklin and Nashville. The village sits about 15 minutes west of downtown Franklin, where the Natchez Trace Parkway meets Leiper's Creek. Its post office dates to 1818, and about 18 acres of the village center were listed as a National Register historic district in 1998.

The village itself is a short strip of restaurants, galleries and shops: the Country Boy Restaurant for breakfast, 1892 for dinner, Puckett's for live music, Fox & Locke for songwriter nights, and a handful of galleries and antique stores. Musicians, artists and executives have been quietly buying farms in the surrounding hills for decades.

The real estate is land. Most luxury properties are farms and estates of 5 to more than 100 acres, with restored farmhouses, new custom homes, barns and pastures. Because the area is unincorporated, owners pay only the Williamson County tax rate.

The market

Home prices in Leiper's Fork

Leiper's Fork land trades slowly and often privately. Pricing depends on acreage, usable (flat) land, road frontage, water, views and the condition of the home and outbuildings, so per-acre comparisons can mislead. Expect a range of about $2M to more than $10M for the properties most luxury buyers look at.

Due diligence matters more here than in a subdivision: surveys, easements, conservation restrictions, well and septic capacity, flood zones near the creeks, greenbelt (agricultural) tax status and what can be built. Plan for a longer search and a longer inspection period.

FAQ

Leiper's Fork questions, answered

Is Leiper's Fork a town?
No. Leiper's Fork is an unincorporated village in Williamson County with about 650 residents. Services and taxes come from the county.
How much does a farm in Leiper's Fork cost?
Most luxury farms and estates trade from about $2M to more than $10M, depending on acreage, usable land, views and improvements.
What should I check before buying land in Leiper's Fork?
Survey and easements, any conservation restrictions, well and septic capacity, flood zones near the creeks, greenbelt tax status and what you can build.
How far is Leiper's Fork from Nashville?
About 35 to 45 minutes to downtown Nashville and about 15 minutes to downtown Franklin, depending on the property.
