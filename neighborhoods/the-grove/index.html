Overview

Living in The Grove

The Grove is the community that put College Grove on the national luxury map. Opened in 2013 on about 1,100 acres of rolling horse country southeast of Franklin, it pairs a private Greg Norman Signature golf course with resort-style amenities: the Manor House clubhouse, a spa, pools, tennis, fitness and an equestrian center.

Membership is part of owning here. Homeowners join at either the Sports or the Golf level, with an initiation at closing and monthly dues on top of the HOA. Published figures have shown about $50,000 (Sports) or $100,000 (Golf) at closing, with dues of about $312 or $907 a month. Confirm current terms with the club before you write an offer.

Because College Grove is unincorporated, homes pay only the Williamson County rate of $1.30 per $100 of assessed value, the lowest property tax in the region's luxury market. That helps offset the club costs for many buyers.

The market

Home prices in The Grove

The Grove sets the top of the College Grove market. 2026 sales have run from about $2M for villa-style homes to about $8M for estates on the course, with an average of about $3.55M. Three of the region's ten highest-priced sales in September 2026 were in College Grove, including Mountaintop Drive at $5.50M and Native Pony Trail at $4.80M.

Many buyers here are relocating from California, the Northeast and Florida. Build-ready lots and new construction are a meaningful share of activity, so compare finished homes against what it would cost to build.

FAQ

The Grove questions, answered

Is membership required at The Grove?
Yes. Homeowners join the club at the Sports or Golf level. Published figures have shown about $50,000 or $100,000 at closing, with monthly dues of about $312 or $907; confirm current terms with the club.
Is The Grove gated?
Yes. The Grove is a gated community of about 1,100 acres.
What schools serve The Grove?
The Grove is in Williamson County Schools and is currently zoned to College Grove Elementary, Fred J. Page Middle and Page High. Confirm the assignment for a specific address with the district.
What are property taxes at The Grove?
College Grove is unincorporated, so homes pay the Williamson County rate of $1.30 per $100 of assessed value with no city tax. Tennessee assesses homes at 25% of appraised value, so a $3M home pays about $9,750 a year.
How much do homes at The Grove cost?
2026 sales have ranged from about $2M to $8M, with an average of about $3.55M.
