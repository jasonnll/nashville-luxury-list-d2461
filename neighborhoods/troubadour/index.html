Overview

Living in Troubadour

Troubadour is Discovery Land Company's Middle Tennessee community, from the developer behind private clubs such as Yellowstone Club and Baker's Bay. Ground broke in 2016 on about 860 acres in College Grove, with about 369 homes planned around a Tom Fazio-designed golf course.

The model is a full-service private club with homes around it, not a neighborhood with a golf course. Membership is required. Published terms have included a $200,000 joining fee, about $40,000 a year in dues and a 1% community fee on purchases, plus HOA dues of about $415 a month. Confirm current terms with the club; they change.

Like The Grove, Troubadour is in unincorporated Williamson County and pays only the county property tax rate. Compared with The Grove, it is smaller, newer and costs more to join, with a more exclusive, club-first feel.

The market

Home prices in Troubadour

Recent Troubadour sales have had a median of about $3.95M, with new construction and custom builds a large share of activity. Because membership costs are high, compare the total cost of ownership with The Grove and with gated communities closer to Franklin and Brentwood.

Inventory is limited and many sales happen through the club's own sales team. A buyer's agent who knows the community can help you weigh resale homes against lots and new builds.

FAQ

Troubadour questions, answered

Is membership required at Troubadour?
Yes. Published terms have included a $200,000 joining fee and about $40,000 a year in dues, plus a 1% community fee on purchases. Confirm current terms with the club.
Who developed Troubadour?
Discovery Land Company, which broke ground in 2016.
How does Troubadour compare with The Grove?
Troubadour is smaller (about 860 acres vs. about 1,100), newer and costs more to join. The Grove has more homes, an equestrian center and a Greg Norman course; Troubadour has a Tom Fazio course and a more club-first model.
What are property taxes at Troubadour?
College Grove is unincorporated, so homes pay only the Williamson County rate of $1.30 per $100 of assessed value.
