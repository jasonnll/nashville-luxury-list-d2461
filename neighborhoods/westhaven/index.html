Overview

Living in Westhaven

Westhaven was built to feel like an old Southern town, and two decades in, it largely does. Southern Land Company started the community in 2003 on farmland just west of downtown Franklin along Highway 96. Homes sit close to the street with front porches and alleys behind, and almost everything is within walking or golf-cart distance of the town center.

That town center is what sets Westhaven apart. More than 30 businesses, including restaurants, coffee, a market, salons and offices, sit inside the neighborhood. Residents share pools, fitness facilities, lakes and about nine miles of trails, and the community calendar runs year-round.

The private Westhaven Golf Club, an Arthur Hills design, sits at the center. Membership is separate from the HOA, so you can live here without joining. Westhaven is not gated; its appeal is walkability and community life rather than seclusion.

The market

Home prices in Westhaven

Westhaven is several markets in one. Townhomes and cottages sit at the entry level, family homes on standard lots fill the middle, and estate homes on the golf course and the larger lots reach the top. Over the 12 months to October 2026, sales ran from about $950K to about $6M, averaging roughly $1.8M.

Walkability carries a premium here. Homes within a few minutes of the town center, or facing a park or the course, trade above similar homes on the edges.

FAQ

Westhaven questions, answered

Is Westhaven gated?
No. Westhaven is an open, walkable master-planned community.
Do you have to join the golf club to live in Westhaven?
No. Westhaven Golf Club is private with its own membership, separate from the HOA.
What are HOA fees in Westhaven?
HOA dues have run about $250 to $265 a month. Confirm current dues and any capital contribution at closing in the HOA documents.
What schools serve Westhaven?
Westhaven is in Williamson County Schools, with Pearre Creek or Hillsboro Elementary, Hillsboro Middle and Independence High. Confirm the assignment for a specific address with the district.
How much do homes in Westhaven cost?
Over the 12 months to October 2026, sales ranged from about $950K to about $6M, with an average of roughly $1.8M.
