Annandale, Brentwood TN

Annandale is a gated Brentwood community of about 180 estate homes on 221 acres, built from 2006 on, with two pools, a cabana, a 14-acre park and walking trails. Entrances are on Cloverland Drive and Old Smyrna Road. Most of it sits in Williamson County, but a small part is in Davidson, so taxes and schools must be checked by parcel.

Overview

Living in Annandale

Annandale is one of the newer estate communities in Brentwood, and it shows in the homes. Construction began in 2006, so most houses here have the open plans, large kitchens, finished lower levels and three-car garages that buyers coming from older Brentwood neighborhoods often end up renovating toward.

The community covers about 221 acres with roughly 180 homes, so lots are generous and the streets feel quiet. Shared amenities include two pools, a cabana, a 14-acre park and walking trails. The entrances are gated, on Cloverland Drive and on Old Smyrna Road.

The detail that matters most for buyers is the county line. Most of Annandale is in Williamson County and the City of Brentwood, but a small portion falls in Davidson County. The side a parcel is on decides its property tax rate and its public schools, so check every home by parcel, not by mailing address.

The market

Home prices in Annandale

Annandale homes generally trade in the upper tier of the Brentwood market, from the high $1 millions into the $4 millions and above for the largest estates. Newer construction and big lots keep demand steady, and well-kept homes here rarely sit long.

When comparing an Annandale home with one elsewhere in Brentwood, adjust for the county side. A Davidson County parcel carries Nashville's tax rate and Metro schools, which changes both the carrying cost and the buyer pool.

FAQ

Annandale questions, answered

Is Annandale in Brentwood gated?
Yes. Annandale has gated entrances on Cloverland Drive and Old Smyrna Road. Ask the HOA how the gates are staffed or monitored today.
Is Annandale in Williamson or Davidson County?
Mostly Williamson County, but a small portion is in Davidson County. Confirm the county for any specific parcel, because it decides the tax rate and the public schools.
When were the homes in Annandale built?
Construction began in 2006, so the homes are among the newer estate homes in Brentwood.
What amenities does Annandale have?
Two pools, a cabana, a 14-acre park and walking trails.
