Nashville's best addresses

Nashville's Most Coveted Neighborhoods

Nashville's luxury neighborhoods span two counties. In Davidson County, Belle Meade, Forest Hills and Oak Hill offer private estate lots minutes from downtown, while Green Hills, 12 South, East Nashville and Sylvan Park are the walkable choices. In Williamson County, Brentwood and Franklin pair top-rated public schools with larger lots, and gated golf communities run from Governors Club in Brentwood to The Grove and Troubadour in College Grove.
