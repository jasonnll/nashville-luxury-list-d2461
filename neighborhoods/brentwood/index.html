Brentwood Neighborhood Guide

Top-ranked schools, large lots, and Williamson County's most consistently coveted address.

Brentwood, Tennessee is the suburb that defined Williamson County's luxury reputation. Twenty minutes south of downtown Nashville along I-65, the city of roughly 45,000 has been Nashville's flagship family destination for forty years—built around top-rated public schools, one-acre minimum lots in most of the city, and a quiet, established character that has aged better than most American suburbs.

What buyers find in Brentwood is consistency. The schools have ranked among Tennessee's best for decades. The neighborhoods—Governors Club, Annandale, River Oaks, Concord Hunt, Witherspoon, Hampton Reserve—are mature, landscaped, and zoned to preserve their feel. The luxury price ceiling has risen, but the floor of what makes Brentwood Brentwood has not moved.

The city's character is shaped by its zoning. Most of Brentwood operates on a one-acre minimum lot size for single-family homes—a holdover from the 1980s that has kept the suburb leafy, low-density, and visually consistent. Newer master-planned communities like Governors Club and Hampton Reserve operate under different rules, but the citywide effect is the same: Brentwood does not feel built-out, even as inventory tightens.

Real Estate

The Brentwood Real Estate Market

Brentwood has stayed firmer than the broader region through 2026. The three-month-to-August median sale price landed at $1.42M (up 5% year-over-year), with price per square foot at $356 (up 9%) and homes closing at 97.4% of list on a median 63 days on market. Brentwood's low city tax rate, combined with Williamson County's lower county rate, keeps the all-in annual tax bill on a $3M home roughly $9,700 to $9,900 lower than the Davidson County equivalent—a meaningful part of why Brentwood commands the premium it does.

Buyers should think of Brentwood in three layers. The $1.2M to $2.5M band covers most established neighborhoods—Concord Hunt, Witherspoon, River Oaks, Maryland Manor—with renovated 1980s to 2000s homes on about an acre. The $2.5M to $3M band moves into larger renovations and entry to gated communities. Above $3M, the market concentrates in Governors Club (a gated golf community with an Arnold Palmer-designed course), Annandale, and custom estates on the city's western and southern edges.

Community

Lifestyle

Brentwood's days run on its parks, clubs and schools: youth sports fields at Crockett Park, dinner at Hill Center and tee times at the area's private golf clubs. Maryland Farms and nearby Cool Springs are major office centers, and Tractor Supply is headquartered in Brentwood.

Downtown Nashville is 20 to 25 minutes away and BNA about 25. Homes are served by Williamson County Schools, consistently rated among the top public school districts in Tennessee.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Brentwood

The questions buyers and residents ask most often about life in Brentwood.

Is Brentwood in Davidson County or Williamson County?

Brentwood sits in Williamson County—directly south of Davidson County (which contains Nashville proper). This is significant because Williamson County operates its own school district (Williamson County Schools) and its own property tax rates, both of which are advantages for many buyers. A few communities near the county line, such as Annandale, straddle it, so confirm taxes and schools by parcel.

What is the difference between Ravenwood and Brentwood High School?

Both are top-ranked Williamson County Schools high schools, but they serve different parts of the city. Brentwood High School sits closer to downtown Brentwood and serves the central and northern neighborhoods. Ravenwood High School is located further south and east. Zoning shifts as the district grows, so confirm the assignment for any specific address with Williamson County Schools.

What is Governors Club?

Governors Club is Brentwood's best-known gated community—a master-planned development built around an Arnold Palmer-designed 18-hole private golf course, with a staffed entrance, clubhouse, tennis and pools. See the full Governors Club guide.
