College Grove Neighborhood Guide

The Grove, Greg Norman golf, and Williamson County's most exclusive private communities.

College Grove is the most rural of Williamson County's luxury addresses—and home to its most exclusive private communities. Southeast of Franklin and roughly forty minutes south of downtown Nashville, the small unincorporated community sits in rolling Tennessee horse country, anchored by The Grove, a gated golf community whose Greg Norman Signature course has put College Grove on the national luxury map, and Troubadour, a newer gated golf community nearby.

What College Grove offers, more than any other Middle Tennessee address, is privacy at scale. Lots run from one to twenty acres and beyond. There are no commercial strips. The private communities are the social and amenity centers, but outside their gates the area remains genuinely rural—horse farms, hardwood ridges, and miles of country roads.

What College Grove buyers are choosing, beyond the homes themselves, is a lifestyle that does not exist closer to Nashville. The pace is slower. The lots are larger. The horse fences are real, not decorative. And the surrounding land remains genuinely rural even as Franklin expands south toward it.

Real Estate

The College Grove & The Grove Real Estate Market

The Grove sets the College Grove market, with homes ranging from about $1.9M for smaller villa lots to $8M for premier estates on the Greg Norman Signature course. Price per square foot in The Grove runs about $566. Many homes run 6,000 square feet and up with five-plus bedrooms and the finishes buyers in this tier expect—custom millwork, imported stone, wine cellars, screened porches. Three of the region's ten highest-priced sales in September 2026 were in College Grove, including Mountaintop Drive at $5.50M and Native Pony Trail at $4.80M.

Outside the gated communities, College Grove offers a different market: horse farms and custom homes on five-to-fifty-acre tracts. Pricing here runs from about $1.5M for smaller acreage to $10M+ for working equestrian properties. Because College Grove is unincorporated, the property tax is the Williamson County rate alone — $1.30 per $100 assessed, the lowest in this region's luxury market. Inventory is extremely thin and off-market activity is significant. Buyers should expect a longer search and plan for private introductions.

Dining

Dining in & Near College Grove

College Grove itself has limited dining—a reflection of its rural character. The private clubhouses anchor in-community dining, and downtown Franklin is the go-to for evening reservations.

  • In-CommunityThe Grove ClubhousePrivate club dining for members, from casual to formal, plus poolside service.
  • LocalTriple Crown BakerySmall-batch bakery and breakfast spot in nearby Thompson's Station.
  • FranklinCork & Cow / Red Pony / Gray's on MainDowntown Franklin's marquee dining is the area's go-to for evening reservations.
  • ResortSouthallLuxury working-farm resort in south Franklin with chef-driven restaurants open to non-guests by reservation.

Community

Lifestyle

Daily life centers on the clubs, the equestrian center and the surrounding countryside: golf, riding or a walk on the property, then dinner at the club or in Franklin. The pace is markedly slower than Nashville or Franklin, and Tennessee has no state income tax on wages.

BNA is 35 to 40 minutes away, and John C. Tune Airport in West Nashville and local FBOs serve private aviation. Homes are served by Williamson County Schools, with private school options in Franklin and Brentwood.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About College Grove

The questions buyers and residents ask most often about life in College Grove.

What is The Grove in College Grove TN?

The Grove is a private, gated luxury golf community in College Grove, Tennessee, anchored by a Greg Norman Signature 18-hole golf course. Homes run from roughly $1.9M to $8M, with an equestrian center, clubhouse, tennis, fitness and resort pools. See the full guide to The Grove.

How far is College Grove from downtown Franklin?

College Grove sits southeast of downtown Franklin, a drive of roughly 20-30 minutes depending on the address. Most College Grove residents consider Franklin their primary town for shopping, dining, and services.

Is there a public school in College Grove?

Yes. College Grove Elementary School is part of the Williamson County Schools district. Older students are typically zoned to Fred J. Page Middle and Page High, also WCS schools a short drive away. Confirm zoning for any specific address with the district.
