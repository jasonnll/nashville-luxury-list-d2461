What is the Friday List?

The Friday List is a free weekly email from Jason Kloess, a REALTOR® with Compass in Nashville, Tennessee. Every Friday it features the 5 best new homes priced at $1.5 million and up across Nashville and Williamson County, each 14 days on market or less and never repeated, with beds, baths, acreage and a direct link. It also shares 3 private or off market homes that subscribers see before they reach public sites like Zillow, plus a short read on luxury pricing, inventory and days on market. Coverage includes Belle Meade, Green Hills, Forest Hills, Oak Hill, 12 South, Brentwood, Franklin, Leiper's Fork, College Grove and Thompson's Station. More than 600 buyers, relocating families, homeowners and investors read it. Signing up takes only an email address, and every issue has a one click unsubscribe.
