Nashville Country Clubs

Nashville's private clubs range from Belle Meade Country Club, founded in 1901, to newer Williamson County clubs such as The Grove and Troubadour, where membership comes with the home. Below are 13 private clubs across the territory, what each offers, which neighborhoods sit closest and how membership works.

  • Belle Meade, Richland and Hillwood anchor the Davidson side
  • Brentwood and Franklin add Governors Club, Vanderbilt Legends, Old Natchez and Temple Hills
  • The Grove and Troubadour require membership for homeowners
  • Most clubs don't publish fees; many need sponsors or have waitlists

Belle Meade Country Club

Founded 1901; at Belle Meade since 1916. Rees Jones renovation, 2003. Nashville's oldest and most traditional club: golf, tennis, pools and dining. Hosted the 1955 U.S. Senior Amateur and is scheduled to host the 2028 U.S. Senior Women's Amateur and the 2036 U.S. Senior Amateur.

Membership

How club membership works here

01

Stand-alone clubs

Belle Meade, Richland, Hillwood, Brentwood CC, Nashville Golf & Athletic, Old Natchez and Temple Hills admit members regardless of where they live. Most require sponsors, and some have waitlists.

02

Optional community clubs

Governors Club and Westhaven have private clubs inside the community. Homeowners can join, but don't have to.

03

Required community clubs

At The Grove and Troubadour, owning a home means joining the club, with initiation at closing and monthly or annual dues.

04

Social vs. golf

Many clubs offer social, sports or tennis-only levels that cost much less than full golf. Ask what each level includes.

05

Timing

If summer swim or junior tennis matters, start the membership conversation when you start the home search.

06

Budget it like a cost of the house

Initiation, dues, food minimums and assessments add up. Put them next to property tax when you compare neighborhoods.

FAQ

Country club questions, answered

What is the most exclusive country club in Nashville?
Belle Meade Country Club is the oldest and most traditional, founded in 1901. In Williamson County, Troubadour and The Grove require membership for homeowners and sit at the top of the cost range.
Do you need to live in a community to join its club?
It depends. Belle Meade, Richland, Hillwood and most stand-alone clubs admit members who live elsewhere. Clubs inside communities, such as Governors Club and Westhaven, are optional for homeowners, while The Grove and Troubadour require homeowners to join.
How much does it cost to join a Nashville country club?
Most clubs do not publish initiation fees or dues, and many have waitlists. Expect a sponsor requirement at the older clubs. Community clubs in College Grove publish more: Troubadour's published joining fee has been about $200,000, and The Grove's about $50,000 to $100,000 depending on level. Confirm current terms with each club.
Which clubs have hosted USGA championships?
Belle Meade hosted the 1955 U.S. Senior Amateur and is scheduled for the 2028 U.S. Senior Women's Amateur and 2036 U.S. Senior Amateur. Hillwood hosted the 2015 U.S. Senior Women's Amateur.
Can I buy a home near a club before getting in?
Yes, and many buyers do. Ask about the club's membership process, sponsors and waitlist early, since timing can matter for families planning around summer swim and tennis.
