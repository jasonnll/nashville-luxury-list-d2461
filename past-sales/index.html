How Jason markets a luxury listing

1

Private exclusive first

Before a home goes public it can be offered as a Compass Private Exclusive, and Jason emails it to every Middle Tennessee agent, more than 9,000 of them, so agents who already have qualified buyers hear about it first.

2

Coming soon, live and open house

The same agent-wide email goes out again at coming soon, on the day it goes live and before each open house.

3

Photography and video, promoted

A professional photo shoot and video, then paid promotion of that video to the buyers most likely to move at this price.

4

Targeted buyer ads

Google, Facebook and Instagram campaigns aimed at luxury buyers in Middle Tennessee and at people relocating here.

5

The Nashville Luxury List

Every listing is featured in Jason's weekly email of the area's best $1.5M+ homes, read by 600+ buyers who are actively looking.
