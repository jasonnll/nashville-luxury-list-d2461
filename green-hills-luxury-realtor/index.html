Hiring an agent

How to judge any agent's claim to be the best in Green Hills

01

Ask for addresses, not career totals

Career totals and badges are hard to compare. Closed addresses are easy to check. Ask any agent for their recent Green Hills closings at your price, with the side they represented. Jason has none in Green Hills yet, and this page says so. His closings elsewhere are in the table above.

02

Check the record yourself

Each sale above comes from Jason's Compass profile, and most prices are marked "Verified by Public Record". You can look up any of them.

03

Look for local fluency

An agent who closes in Green Hills regularly should know its blocks, its rules and what the last few sales mean for your price without looking it up.

04

Get the plan in writing

Sellers should see exactly what happens in the first two weeks: private exclusive or not, who hears about the home first, the photo and video budget, and where the ads run. Buyers should ask how often the agent's clients see homes before they reach the portals.

What the Green Hills market looks like now

$1.25MMedian sale price3 mo. to Jun 2026 · +14% YoY
$439Median price per sq ft+22% YoY
62 daysMedian time on market3 mo. to Jun 2026
96.9%Sale-to-list ratio3 mo. to Jun 2026

Green Hills has been one of the strongest Davidson County submarkets this year: a $1.25 million median, up 14% year over year, and $439 a square foot, up 22%. Homes closed at a median 96.9% of list price after 62 days on the market, so buyers have typically had some room to negotiate.

The range is wider than buyers expect. A renovated 1950s brick ranch on a quarter-acre lot can list in the $900Ks, while new construction on streets such as Estes Road or Lynnwood Boulevard regularly clears $3 million. The most competitive segment is single-family homes from $1.2 million to $1.9 million. Above about $2.5 million, the market is mostly tear-down-and-rebuild projects where the lot justifies a custom home.

The Mall at Green Hills, Hillsboro Pike shops, the Bluebird Cafe and Radnor Lake State Park are all close, Vanderbilt and Midtown are about 8 to 10 minutes away, and downtown is about 12 minutes off-peak.
