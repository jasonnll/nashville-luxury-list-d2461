Hiring an agent

How to judge any agent's claim to be the best in 12 South

01

Ask for addresses, not career totals

Career totals and badges are hard to compare. Closed addresses are easy to check. Ask any agent for their recent 12 South closings at your price, with the side they represented. Jason's are in the table above.

02

Check the record yourself

Each sale above comes from Jason's Compass profile, and most prices are marked "Verified by Public Record". You can look up any of them.

03

Look for local fluency

An agent who closes in 12 South regularly should know its blocks, its rules and what the last few sales mean for your price without looking it up.

04

Get the plan in writing

Sellers should see exactly what happens in the first two weeks: private exclusive or not, who hears about the home first, the photo and video budget, and where the ads run. Buyers should ask how often the agent's clients see homes before they reach the portals.

12 South was a forgotten strip of South Nashville until the mid-2000s. Now it is anchored by the I Believe in Nashville mural and a six-block stretch of independent boutiques, restaurants and coffee shops, with blocks of restored Craftsman bungalows, Tudor cottages and tall-skinny new builds behind it.

The $1.2M to $2M+ tier is mostly new construction and restored bungalows; above about $1.8 million you move into the premier new builds on the best blocks. Lots are small, so privacy, parking and the build quality of a new home matter more than square footage.
