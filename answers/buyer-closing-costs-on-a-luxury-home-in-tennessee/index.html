What are buyer closing costs on a $2 million home in Tennessee?

Tennessee adds two state taxes that buyers usually pay at closing. The realty transfer tax is $0.37 per $100 of the price, or $7,400 on a $2 million home, and is customarily the buyer's. If you finance, the mortgage (indebtedness) tax is $0.115 per $100 of the loan above the first $2,000, or about $1,838 on a $1,600,000 loan. On top of those come the lender's title policy, lender fees, inspections and an escrow deposit for property tax and insurance. Cash buyers skip the mortgage tax and lender costs.

  • Transfer tax: $7,400 on $2M (0.37%)
  • Mortgage tax: about $1,838 on a $1,600,000 loan (0.115% above $2,000)
  • Tennessee has no state income tax, and no separate city or county transfer tax
  • Property tax is prorated at closing and depends heavily on the county

FAQ

Questions, answered

How much is the transfer tax in Tennessee?
$0.37 per $100 of the price (or value, if greater). On a $2 million home that is $7,400. It is customarily paid by the buyer.
How much is the mortgage tax in Tennessee?
$0.115 per $100 of the loan, with the first $2,000 exempt. On a $1,600,000 loan it is about $1,838.
Do cash buyers pay the mortgage tax?
No. The indebtedness tax applies only to recorded debt.
Can the seller pay my closing costs?
It can be negotiated. Seller credits toward closing costs are common on new construction and in slower markets; with about five months of $1M+ supply in fall 2026, buyers have more room to ask.
