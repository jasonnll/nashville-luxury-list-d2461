How do Compass Private Exclusives work in Nashville?

A Compass Private Exclusive is a home a seller has listed with a Compass agent but not yet marketed publicly. It is shared agent to agent, inside Compass and with other agents who have a buyer, but it does not appear on Zillow or the MLS. It is usually the first of three steps: Private Exclusive, then Coming Soon, then a full public launch. Sellers use it to test price and gather feedback quietly; the trade-off is a smaller audience while the home is private. Buyers get access through an agent who hears about these homes, or through lists such as the Friday Nashville Luxury List, which includes three private or off-market homes each week.

  • Private Exclusive: shared agent to agent, not on Zillow or the MLS

  • Seller benefit: test price and feedback before the public sees a price history
  • Trade-off: fewer buyers see it while it is private, so it suits some homes and not others

The background

Why private listings are in the news

The National Association of REALTORS® Clear Cooperation Policy requires a listing to go on the MLS within one business day of public marketing. In 2025 NAR added a "delayed marketing" option that lets sellers keep a listing off public sites for a period while it is in the MLS. Compass has pushed back on Clear Cooperation and continues to offer private listings; listing portals such as Zillow have supported the policy. The rules and how each local MLS enforces them are still changing, so ask your agent exactly how a private period would work for your home in Middle Tennessee.

For sellers

When a private start makes sense

It tends to help when the right price is uncertain, when privacy matters (a well-known owner, a home that should not show up online with a price history), or when the likely buyers are already known to local luxury agents. It is less useful when the home needs the widest possible audience on day one.

Jason's launch plan starts this way when it fits: a Private Exclusive or Coming Soon period to test price, an email to 9,000+ Middle Tennessee agents at each stage, then professional photography and video, the Friday Nashville Luxury List and a targeted ads launch when the home goes public.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can anyone buy a Compass Private Exclusive?
Yes. A Private Exclusive is marketed agent to agent, and buyers working with agents at other brokerages can buy one. You have to hear about it first, which is why your agent's network matters.
What is the difference between a Private Exclusive and Coming Soon?
A Private Exclusive is shared privately between agents. Coming Soon is a public signal that a home is about to launch, before showings and the full MLS listing begin.
Do Private Exclusives sell for less?
There is no settled answer. Brokerages and portals have argued both sides. A private period can protect a home's price history; it also limits how many buyers see it. The right choice depends on the home and the market.
