What does it cost to sell a $2 million home in Nashville?

Most of the cost of selling a luxury home in Nashville is commission, which is negotiated with your agent and with the buyer's side rather than fixed by law. The rest is smaller: the owner's title insurance policy (customarily the seller's in Middle Tennessee), your share of the year's property tax up to closing, attorney or closing fees, and anything you spend preparing the home. Tennessee's realty transfer tax, $0.37 per $100 of the price ($7,400 on $2 million), is customarily paid by the buyer, though the contract can say otherwise.

  • Commission: negotiable; ask for it in writing, split by side
  • Transfer tax: $7,400 on a $2M sale, customarily the buyer's in Tennessee
  • Prorated property tax: on a $2M Green Hills home the full-year bill is about $14,070
  • Prep: Compass Concierge can front the cost of improvements, repaid at closing (terms apply)

Lowering the cost

Where sellers actually save

The largest number to manage is not a fee. It is the gap between list and sale price. In the $1 million-plus market, 43% of active listings were below their original asking price at the end of September 2026, so pricing correctly at launch usually saves more than any fee negotiation.

A quiet start can help with that: a Compass Private Exclusive or Coming Soon period lets you test price with agents and buyers before the home is public, so the first public price is one the market has already reacted to. See how Private Exclusives work.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Who pays the transfer tax when you sell a house in Tennessee?
The realty transfer tax of $0.37 per $100 of the sale price is customarily paid by the buyer in Tennessee, but the purchase contract can assign it to either side. On a $2 million sale it is $7,400.
Who pays for title insurance in Nashville?
In Middle Tennessee the seller customarily pays for the owner's title policy on an existing home, and the buyer pays for the lender's policy. On new construction the buyer often pays for both. It is negotiable.
Are real estate commissions fixed in Nashville?
No. Commissions are negotiated between you and your agent, and any payment to the buyer's agent is a separate negotiation. Get both in writing.
Do I pay capital gains tax to Tennessee when I sell?
Tennessee has no state income tax on wages or investment income. Federal capital gains rules still apply; ask your tax adviser about the home-sale exclusion.
