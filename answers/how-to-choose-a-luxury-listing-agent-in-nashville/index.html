How do I choose a listing agent for a $2M+ home in Nashville?

Choose on evidence you can check, not on titles. Ask each agent for their recent closings at your price and in your area, with the address, price and the side they represented, then look them up. Ask for the launch plan in writing: whether the home starts privately, who hears about it first, the photo and video budget, where ads run and for how long. Then compare pricing. The agent who suggests the highest price is not always the one who sells for the most.

  • Ask for addresses, not career totals or badges
  • Get the first two weeks of the plan in writing
  • Ask how they would price it, and which sales support that price
  • Check reviews on Google and Zillow, not only on their website

The questions

Six questions to ask every agent

01

What have you sold near my home, at my price?

Ask for addresses, prices, dates and which side they represented, then look them up on the county record or their brokerage profile.

02

What happens in the first two weeks?

Private or public start, agent outreach, photography and video, the ads plan, and the first open house.

03

How would you price it, and why?

Ask for the three to five sales that support the number, and what they would do if there are no offers in 21 days.

04

Who does the work?

Who prices, shows, negotiates and answers your calls. Meet that person.

05

What does it cost?

Commission split by side, marketing you pay for directly, and the length of the listing agreement.

06

What do past clients say?

Read Google and Zillow reviews, and ask for two clients you can call.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Who is the best luxury realtor in Nashville?
There is no official ranking, and many "best realtor" lists are written or paid for by the agents on them. Compare agents on recent, checkable closings at your price and on their written plan.
Should I use a big team or an individual agent?
Either can work. Ask who will actually price the home, run the showings and negotiate the offers, and meet that person.
