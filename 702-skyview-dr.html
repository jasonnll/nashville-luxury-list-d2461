The Skyview Compound

Room for everyone, behind your own gate

Behind a private gate on one of East Nashville's most walkable streets, 702 Skyview Drive pairs a 2015-built main residence with a fully separate guest apartment on one fenced lot. With 4,467 square feet, five bedrooms, five full and two half baths and a 2.5-car garage, it's built for the way people live now: space for extended family, long-stay guests, a caregiver or a home office that never shares a wall with the kitchen.

Inside the main home, a private suite with its own living room and kitchen gives in-laws, an adult child or visiting family real independence while staying connected to the rest of the house. Across the property, the two-bedroom, two-bath apartment has its own entrance and full kitchen, ready for guests or for rental income, long-term or short-term (buyer to verify permitting).

Step outside the gate and East Nashville's restaurants, coffee shops and neighborhood favorites are a short walk away, with downtown about ten minutes by car.
