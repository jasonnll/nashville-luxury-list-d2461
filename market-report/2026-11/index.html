Nashville Luxury Market Report: November 2026

Closings and new contracts slowed in October while listings kept climbing.

  • Across nine Middle Tennessee counties, 2,540 homes closed in October, 12% fewer than a year earlier, and pending sales fell 21% to 1,805.
  • 15,379 homes were for sale, about six months of supply at the current pace, according to Greater Nashville Realtors.
  • Williamson County had 1,665 active listings in October, up 18.6% from a year earlier; Davidson County had 4,621, up 11.1%.
  • The nine-county median sale price was $499,990, and the median condo price was $343,776.

Luxury

The luxury market

No October count of $1M+ closings or listings had been published when this edition was prepared, so this month leans on county listing data. Williamson County is a reasonable stand-in for the luxury tier: its median listing price was about $1.16M in September, so roughly half of its listings are priced above $1.1M. Williamson had 1,665 active listings in October, up 18.6% from a year earlier, and has held above 1,650 every month since July.

Across the nine-county region, pending sales fell 21% from a year earlier, a sharper drop than closings (down 12%). Contracts signed now become closings over the next month or two, so closings are likely to stay soft into the end of the year. With about six months of supply overall, buyers have more room to negotiate than they did a year ago, and sellers are competing with more listings.

What it means

What this means for you

If you're buying

  • → Six months of supply and a 21% drop in contracts give you room to negotiate on price, repairs and closing costs.
  • → Ask how long a home has been listed and whether the price has been cut; the longer it has sat, the more room there usually is.
  • → Well-priced homes in the most sought-after locations still sell, so have your financing ready before you tour.

If you're selling

  • → Price to recent closed sales, not to what neighbors listed for in the spring.
  • → With more listings to choose from, condition and presentation decide which homes get the showings.
  • → If you can wait, a private pre-market launch lets you test the price before the home goes public.
