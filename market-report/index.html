Nashville Luxury Market Report

A dated, monthly read on the luxury market across Davidson and Williamson counties. Latest edition: October 2026, with data through September 2026. More homes, fewer closings: luxury buyers have leverage heading into winter.

  • Regional inventory reached about 16,400 homes in September, up 3% from a year earlier, while closings fell 10%.
  • About 2,289 homes priced at $1M+ were for sale against roughly 460 closings a month, about five months of supply.
  • 43% of active $1M+ listings were priced below their original asking price.
  • Williamson County's median listing price held near $1.16M while its active listings rose about 15% year over year.

Method

How this report is built

Each edition is published monthly and dated, using the most recent full month of data available. Regional figures come from Realtracs, the Middle Tennessee MLS. County listing prices and inventory come from Realtor.com data published by the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. Neighborhood medians come from Redfin's MLS-based data.

Nashville Luxury List defines luxury as $1.5 million and up; some measures here use $1 million and up, where that's how the source reports them. Sources are linked in every edition.
