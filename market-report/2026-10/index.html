Nashville Luxury Market Report: October 2026

More homes, fewer closings: luxury buyers have leverage heading into winter.

  • Regional inventory reached about 16,400 homes in September, up 3% from a year earlier, while closings fell 10%.
  • About 2,289 homes priced at $1M+ were for sale against roughly 460 closings a month, about five months of supply.
  • 43% of active $1M+ listings were priced below their original asking price.
  • Williamson County's median listing price held near $1.16M while its active listings rose about 15% year over year.

Luxury

The $1M+ market

Across the region, 1,380 homes priced at $1M or more closed from June through August, about 460 a month. At the end of September roughly 2,289 were for sale. At the current pace that is about five months of supply, which leans toward buyers in the luxury tier even as the overall market stays balanced.

Pricing is the clearest signal. 43% of active $1M+ listings were below their original asking price, so sellers who priced aggressively in spring are adjusting. Well-priced homes in the best locations are still selling quickly.

What it means

What this means for you

If you're buying

  • → Five months of $1M+ supply means room to negotiate, especially on homes that have already cut price.
  • → Ask for days on market and the price history on every home; 43% have reduced.
  • → The best-priced homes in Belle Meade, Brentwood and Franklin still move fast, so be ready.

If you're selling

  • → Price to the last 90 days of sales, not to spring's asking prices.
  • → Presentation and launch matter more when buyers have choices.
  • → Consider a private pre-market launch to test price before going public.
