Questions

Nashville luxury real estate, answered

Short, dated answers to what buyers and sellers ask most. See every neighborhood FAQ

What counts as a luxury home in Nashville?

Nashville Luxury List defines luxury as homes priced at $1.5 million and above, more than three times the citywide median of about $476,000.

Is the Nashville housing market cooling in 2026?

Citywide prices are essentially flat, down about 0.5% year over year through August 2026, with a median of 64 days to sell. Some luxury submarkets are still rising, including Brentwood (up 5.1%) and Franklin (up 12.4%).

Which neighborhoods have the most expensive homes?

Belle Meade leads with a median sale of about $4.3 million for the three months ending August 2026 and about $880 per square foot. Forest Hills and Green Hills follow, with Brentwood leading Williamson County.

Do luxury buyers have negotiating room right now?

Some. About 43% of active $1M+ listings are priced below their original ask, and the $1M+ segment has about five months of supply. Well-priced, move-in-ready homes in top locations still sell near list.

How do I see off-market homes in Nashville?

Many sellers at this level use Compass Private Exclusives and Coming Soon, which are shown to agents and their buyers before or instead of a public launch. Nashville Luxury List members get three of them every Friday by email, free.

Is Brentwood or Franklin more expensive?

Brentwood. Its median of about $1.42 million is roughly 65% higher than Franklin's $863,000, and its price per square foot is $356 versus $317, for the three months ending August 2026.
