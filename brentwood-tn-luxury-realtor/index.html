Hiring an agent

How to choose the best luxury realtor in Brentwood

Most Brentwood luxury buyers are relocating or moving up from Nashville, and most sellers are competing with new construction a few miles south. Here is what to ask any agent before you hire them.

01

Do they check the county line?

Annandale and a few other communities sit on or near the Davidson–Williamson line. The side a parcel falls on decides its tax rate and its schools, so your agent should confirm it by parcel, not by mailing address.

02

Do they verify school zoning by address?

Brentwood is in Williamson County Schools, served by Brentwood and Ravenwood high schools, and zones shift as the district grows. A listing's school field is a starting point, not an answer.

03

Do they know the community rules?

Gated communities such as Governors Club, Annandale and Rosebrooke carry HOA rules, fees and architectural review that shape what you can build, fence or add. Read them before the offer, not after.

04

Can they price against new construction?

Resale Brentwood homes compete with new builds on one-acre homesites in communities such as Calistoga and Tiburon. Pricing has to account for what a buyer could build instead.

05

How will your home reach Nashville and out-of-state buyers?

Many Brentwood buyers are coming from Nashville or another state. Ask how the agent reaches them before the home goes public, and what the ad plan is once it does.

06

Is the advice on timing honest?

Brentwood homes took a median of 63 days to sell and closed about 3% under list on average. The right agent tells you what that means for your price and your move date.

What this means for you

  • Property tax: on a $3M home, about $11,175 a year at Brentwood's $1.49 rate, against about $12,150 in Franklin and $21,105 in Nashville's Urban Services District. Over ten years, the gap with Nashville is close to six figures.
  • Land: Brentwood's low-density zoning means many neighborhoods were platted with lots of about an acre. That is what buyers are paying for, more than square footage.
  • Drive: downtown Nashville is typically 15 to 20 minutes via I-65 off-peak, and Cool Springs about 10 minutes south. The Walk Score is 4, so plan on driving everywhere.
Where to look

Brentwood's luxury neighborhoods

Brentwood's top end splits between guard-gated communities, established estate streets and a new wave of boutique enclaves on one-acre-plus homesites.

Gated · Golf

Governors Club

Brentwood's best-known gated community, spread over about 600 acres of rolling hills with a private golf course and custom estate homes.

Gated · Franklin Road

Annandale

Estate homes behind a 24-hour guarded gate, built mostly from 2006 to 2017, with a pool and walking trails. It straddles the county line, so confirm taxes and schools by parcel.

Gated · Sunset Road

Rosebrooke

Large homes on half-acre to two-acre lots, many with private pools, and consistent design standards throughout.

Established

Taramore & Brenthaven

Established neighborhoods with generous, mature lots: Taramore off Franklin Road and Brenthaven north of Old Hickory Boulevard, close to Maryland Farms.

Near Radnor Lake

Woodward Hills

A small gated enclave of about 15 homes on lots of more than an acre, minutes from Radnor Lake State Park.

New construction

Old Smyrna & Sam Donald Roads

Boutique new communities such as Harlan, Calistoga and Tiburon, with custom homes on one-acre-plus homesites, often from about $1.8M to $2.4M and up.

Brentwood luxury real estate FAQ

Questions buyers and sellers ask first

Who is a good luxury realtor in Brentwood, TN?
Look for an agent who works the $1.5M+ tier in Williamson County, can show you recent closings, and checks school zoning and the county line by parcel. Jason Kloess of Compass represents Brentwood buyers and sellers as part of his Williamson County focus; his closed sales include 1005 Paris Avenue in Nashville at $2.45 million and 2939 Ave Downs Drive in nearby Thompson's Station at $1.56 million.
What does a luxury home cost in Brentwood, TN?
Brentwood's median sale price was $1,424,058 for the three months ending August 2026, up 5.1% from a year earlier. Luxury homes typically run from about $1.2 million to $6 million and up, and sales above $2 million are common in the estate neighborhoods and gated communities.
What are the gated communities in Brentwood?
Governors Club is the best known, with a private golf course on about 600 acres. Others include Annandale, Rosebrooke, Woodward Hills and smaller enclaves such as Harlan off Old Smyrna Road. Each has its own HOA rules and fees.
Is property tax lower in Brentwood than in Nashville?
Yes, by about half. Tennessee assesses homes at 25% of appraised value. On a $3 million home that is about $11,175 a year at Brentwood's 2026 rate, compared with about $21,105 in Nashville's Urban Services District. Confirm any specific home's bill with the county trustee.
Which schools serve Brentwood?
Brentwood is part of Williamson County Schools and is served by Brentwood High and Ravenwood High. Kenrose, Scales, Lipscomb, Jordan and Edmondson elementary schools each hold a 9/10 GreatSchools rating. Zoning changes as the district grows, so confirm the assignment for any specific address with the district.
How long do Brentwood homes take to sell?
The median was 63 days for the three months ending August 2026, and homes sold for 97.4% of list price. The most in-demand homes went pending in about five weeks.
Brentwood or Franklin: which is better for a luxury buyer?
Brentwood is closer to Nashville and more expensive, with a median of about $1.42 million against Franklin's $863,000, and larger typical lots. Franklin offers a walkable historic downtown, more new construction and estate farms toward Leiper's Fork. See the Franklin page for a side-by-side.
How much does a luxury realtor cost?
Commission is negotiable and is set in writing. Sellers agree to it in the listing agreement. Since the 2024 NAR settlement, buyers also sign a written agreement with their agent before touring that states the agent's compensation; it is often paid by the seller as a negotiated term, but that is not guaranteed.
