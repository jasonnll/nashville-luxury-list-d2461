Questions

Common questions

Can I buy a home without my name on the public record?

Deeds in Tennessee are public, but many high-profile buyers take title through a trust or LLC set up by their attorney so their name is not the one that appears. Jason coordinates the timing and paperwork with your attorney and business manager so the structure is in place before closing.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. Jason signs your NDA, or provides one, before any personal details are shared, and keeps showings, offers and communications on a need-to-know basis.

I was just traded or signed. How fast can I find a place?

Short-term furnished leases can often be lined up within days so you can report on time. Purchases typically take 30 to 45 days to close with financing and can be faster with cash. Many players lease for the first season, then buy once they know where they want to live.

Can I buy while I'm in season or out of state?

Yes. Jason previews homes in person, sends private video walkthroughs, and handles inspections, appraisal and closing so you can buy remotely and sign electronically, with your business manager copied as you prefer.

Does Tennessee have a state income tax?

Tennessee does not tax wage income. How that affects your situation, including residency and taxes owed in other states where you play, is a question for your CPA or tax advisor.

Do you work with agents and business managers?

Yes. Many first conversations come from a representative. Jason can work through your agent, business manager or family office with one point of contact and reporting in whatever format they prefer.

Jason Kloess is a licensed real estate agent with Compass and does not give legal or tax advice. Questions about titling, trusts, LLCs and taxes should go to your attorney and CPA; Jason works alongside them.
