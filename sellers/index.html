Before you list

Three decisions that set your price

01

Price to now, not to spring

Buyers compare your home with this month's sales and every price cut around you. The first two weeks matter most.

02

Fix what buyers see first

Paint, lighting, flooring, landscaping and staging usually return the most. Compass Concierge can front the cost.*

03

Choose how to launch

Start private as a Compass Private Exclusive to test price without days on market, or go straight to the MLS. Jason will recommend which fits your home.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How much is my Nashville home worth?
Request a valuation at /home-valuation/. Jason prices from this month's closed and pending sales in your neighborhood, not an algorithm, and adjusts for condition, lot and the competition you'll face.
How should I price a luxury home right now?
Price to the last 90 days of sales. In September 2026, 43% of active $1M+ listings in the region were below their original asking price, so homes priced to spring's numbers are adjusting. Well-priced homes in the best locations still sell quickly.
How do you market a luxury home?
A Compass Private Exclusive or Coming Soon launch announced to 9,000+ Middle Tennessee agents, professional photography and video, targeted Google, Facebook and Instagram ads, and the weekly Nashville Luxury List sent to 600+ buyers.
Can I improve my home before listing without paying up front?
Yes. Compass Concierge fronts the cost of staging, paint, flooring and 100+ other services, with zero due until closing.* Terms apply; see the Compass Concierge page.
Who pays the transfer tax when selling in Tennessee?
By custom the buyer pays Tennessee's transfer tax of $0.37 per $100 of price, though it's negotiable. Sellers typically pay their agent's commission, deed preparation and prorated property tax; the exact split is set in the contract.
