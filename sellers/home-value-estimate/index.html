How this estimate works

It multiplies your square footage by the median sale price per square foot for your area over the latest three months, then adjusts for condition, lot and features. The result is a range, not an appraisal: two homes on the same street can differ by hundreds of thousands of dollars on light, layout, finishes and the specific lot.

Area data: Three months ending October 2026, all home types, from Redfin calculations of MLS and public-record data (retrieved November 18, 2026). Days on market is Redfin’s reported average. Forest Hills, Oak Hill, Thompson’s Station and Nolensville are the cities of those names; College Grove is ZIP 37046 and East Nashville is ZIP 37206. Belle Meade, Forest Hills and Oak Hill have few sales a month, so their figures can swing widely. Updated with the November 2026 Nashville Luxury Index.

What a pricing read adds
