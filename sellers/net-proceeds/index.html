The assumptions

  • Property tax is paid in arrears in Tennessee, so sellers usually credit the buyer their share of the year up to closing: appraised value × 25% × the local rate, prorated by month.
  • Transfer tax ($0.37 per $100) is customarily the buyer’s but can be negotiated.
  • Owner’s title policy is customarily the seller’s in Middle Tennessee. The estimate here is rough; your title company quotes the real premium.
