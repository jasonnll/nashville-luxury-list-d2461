How it works

Three stages, one launch

01

Pick the projects that pay

Jason walks the home and recommends the improvements most likely to return more than they cost, based on recent sales.

02

Start as a Private Exclusive

While work is underway, the home can be shown privately to build early demand and test price, without days on market or public price changes.

03

Launch as Coming Soon

As the work nears completion, the home goes live on Compass.com as Coming Soon to build momentum.

04

Go live everywhere

Once the project is finished, the home goes to the MLS and third-party sites with a refined price and positioning.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Compass Concierge?
A Compass program that fronts the cost of home-improvement services before you sell, such as staging, painting, flooring and landscaping, with nothing due until closing.* It's available to sellers who list with Compass.
Is there interest?
Compass says there's zero due until closing, but depending on your state of residence, fees or interest may apply. Concierge Capital loans are provided by Notable Finance, LLC and are subject to credit approval; terms are in the loan agreement.
When do I repay it?
At the earliest of: the sale of the home, the end of your listing agreement with Compass, 12 months from the Concierge start date, or suspension of the loan under the loan agreement.
Which improvements are worth it?
Usually the ones buyers see first: paint, lighting, flooring, landscaping and staging. Jason recommends projects based on what comparable homes sold for with and without them.
Can I market the home while work is underway?
Yes. Many sellers start as a Compass Private Exclusive, move to Coming Soon as the work wraps up, then go live on the MLS when the home is ready, without accruing days on market in the meantime.
