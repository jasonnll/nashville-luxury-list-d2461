Hiring an agent

How to choose the best luxury realtor in Nashville

There is no official ranking of Nashville's luxury agents, and "best" lists are usually written by the agents on them. These are the questions that separate an agent who works the $1.5M+ tier from one who occasionally lands a big listing.

01

Ask for addresses, not career totals

Career volume hides what an agent has done lately and at what price. Ask for recent closings at your price point, in your part of town. Jason's are listed on this page.

02

Look for submarket fluency

Belle Meade is its own city with its own building review. Green Hills sits in the Urban Services District and Oak Hill in the General Services District, which changes your tax bill. Public school zoning is set by address, not by listing. Your agent should know all of this without looking it up.

03

Get the listing plan in writing

For sellers, ask exactly what happens in the first two weeks: private exclusive or not, who hears about the home before it goes public, what the photo and video budget is, and where the ads run.

04

Ask how they find homes that aren't public

A meaningful share of top-end trades happen as Compass Private Exclusives or quietly between agents. Ask how often the agent's buyers see a home before it reaches the portals.

05

Expect honest pricing

With about five months of $1M+ supply and 43% of active listings below their original ask, the agent who quotes the highest number is not doing you a favor. Ask for the comparable sales behind the price.

06

Know who answers the phone

On a big team, the agent who wins your business may not be the one who shows the home or negotiates the contract. Jason answers his own phone and handles each client himself.

The market · fall 2026

What is the Nashville luxury market doing right now?

As of fall 2026 the upper end of Middle Tennessee is more balanced than it was in 2021 and 2022. Homes take longer to sell and most close a few percent under list, but well-located, well-finished homes still move quickly, often before they reach the public sites.

What this means for you

  • For sellers: price to the comparable sales, not to the hope. Homes that sit get discounted twice, once by the price cut and again by days on market.
  • For buyers: there is room to negotiate on homes that have sat. Closing date, rent-back and repairs often matter as much as price.
  • For both: Nashville luxury is a dozen small markets. Belle Meade's median is more than three times Green Hills', and its price per square foot is roughly double.

Nashville luxury real estate FAQ

Questions buyers and sellers ask first

Who is the best luxury realtor in Nashville?
There is no official ranking, so judge agents on evidence: recent closings at your price point in your area, a written marketing plan, and who will actually handle your sale or search. Jason Kloess of Compass is one Nashville luxury realtor with documented sales up to $2.45 million, including 1005 Paris Avenue in 12 South, and he publishes the weekly Nashville Luxury List of $1.5M+ homes.
What price is considered a luxury home in Nashville?
Nashville Luxury List defines luxury as homes priced at $1.5 million and above, more than three times Nashville's citywide median sale price of about $476,000 for the three months ending August 2026.
Which Nashville neighborhoods have the most expensive homes?
Belle Meade leads, with a median sale of about $4.3 million for the three months ending August 2026. Forest Hills, Oak Hill and Green Hills follow inside Davidson County, and Brentwood leads in Williamson County at about $1.42 million.
How much does a luxury realtor cost?
Commission is negotiable and is set in writing. Sellers agree to it in the listing agreement. Since the 2024 NAR settlement, buyers also sign a written agreement with their agent before touring that states the agent's compensation; it is often paid by the seller as a negotiated term, but that is not guaranteed. Jason walks through the options on your first call.
How do I find off-market luxury homes in Nashville?
Work with an agent who sees Compass Private Exclusives and hears about homes agent-to-agent before they are listed, and tell them your criteria early. Jason's weekly Nashville Luxury List email also features homes before they reach the public sites; you can join it free at Off-Market Access.
Is it a good time to sell a luxury home in Nashville?
It is a balanced market. In fall 2026 there were about five months of supply at $1M and above, and 43% of active regional listings were priced below their original ask. Well-located, well-finished homes priced to the comparable sales still sell quickly; overpriced homes sit. A free valuation is the place to start.
