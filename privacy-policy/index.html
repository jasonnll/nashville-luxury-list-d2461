Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Nashville Luxury List ("we," "us," or "our") operates nashvilleluxurylist.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our Site or submit information through our forms.

By using the Site you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Property address (for home valuation requests)
  • Purchase or sale intent, budget range, preferred neighborhoods
  • Messages or inquiries you submit via contact forms

Information collected automatically

  • IP address and general geographic location
  • Browser type, device type, operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, referral source
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 6)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and fulfill your service requests
  • Prepare and deliver home valuations and market reports
  • Send our VIP weekly digest (only if you opt in)
  • Improve the Site's content and functionality
  • Comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud
  • Analyze traffic patterns via Google Analytics 4
  • Serve relevant advertising via Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Pixel

We will never sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Sharing Your Information

We may share information with:

  • Compass Tennessee, LLC — our brokerage, for transaction and licensing purposes
  • Netlify, Inc. — our web host and form processor
  • Google LLC — analytics (Google Analytics 4)
  • Meta Platforms, Inc. — advertising pixel (if enabled)
  • Email / CRM providers — to deliver newsletters you request
  • Law enforcement or regulators — when required by law

5. Data Retention

We retain form submissions and lead data for up to 3 years, or as long as necessary to provide you with real estate services. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 8).

6. Cookies & Tracking

Our Site uses:

  • Essential cookies — required for the Site to function
  • Analytics cookies — Google Analytics 4, for aggregate traffic analysis
  • Advertising pixels — Meta Pixel, to measure ad effectiveness

You can disable non-essential cookies in your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect Site functionality.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS/TLS encryption, strict HTTP headers (HSTS, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options), and access controls. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

You may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Request that we not sell your data (we do not sell data)
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (EU/UK residents)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at jason@nashvilleluxurylist.com.

9. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. External Links

Our Site may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., Compass, Zillow). We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
